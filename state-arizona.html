📍 About Arizona

The Grand Canyon State's New Chip Boom.

Arizona has transformed from a copper-and-tourism economy into a technology and manufacturing hub. Metro Phoenix is now one of America's fastest-growing semiconductor centers, with TSMC's multibillion-dollar fabs and a long-standing Intel presence, alongside strong aerospace and defense, healthcare, and construction sectors. Tucson adds optics and defense, tourism remains huge — from the Grand Canyon to Scottsdale resorts — and the state's low costs continue to draw businesses and residents from California.

The AZ tax picture

Arizona is one of the friendliest states for income tax: it levies a flat 2.5% individual income tax, the lowest flat rate in the country (as of 2026 — confirm the current rate before filing). Sales tax works differently here — Arizona's Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT) is technically a tax on the vendor's privilege of doing business, not a tax on the buyer, and businesses must hold a TPT license. The state rate is 5.6%, but county and city add-ons bring the combined average to roughly 8.4%, and some jurisdictions top 11%. Many Arizona cities also tax business activities the state does not, so knowing your local TPT obligations matters.
