The MN Tax Angle

A high income tax — and a stacked sales tax.

Minnesota businesses in St. Louis County navigate a graduated income tax that reaches 9.85% and a 6.875% state sales tax, plus local option taxes on top — and a statewide 50-cent Retail Delivery Fee on many deliveries of $100 or more. In the Twin Cities metro, an extra regional 1% sales tax for transit and housing also applies. We keep your income, sales, and delivery-fee records clean.
