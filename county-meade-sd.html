📍 About Meade County

The Rally County.

Meade County is the largest county in South Dakota by area, in the northern Black Hills and prairie — a ranching and tourism economy anchored by Sturgis and its world-famous motorcycle rally.

We serve businesses across the county — Sturgis, and the smaller communities in between.

The SD Tax Angle

No income tax at all — and a low 4.2% sales tax.

South Dakota charges no personal or corporate income tax, and its state sales tax is a low 4.2% that Meade County cities can top with up to 2% municipal tax (about 6.2% combined). We keep your sales and municipal-tax records clean — with no state income-tax return to file.
