📍 About Park County

The Yellowstone County.

Park County is the eastern gateway to Yellowstone National Park in northwest Wyoming — a tourism, hospitality, and ranching economy anchored by Cody, the town Buffalo Bill founded.

We serve businesses across the county — Cody, and the smaller communities in between.

The WY Tax Angle

No income tax at all — and a low 4% sales tax.

Wyoming charges no personal or corporate income tax, and its state sales tax is a low 4% that Park County can top with up to 2% in local-option sales tax (about 5–6% combined). We keep your sales and use-tax records clean — with no state income-tax return to file.
