Where you're starting

Nothing here is broken. This is a handoff, not a rescue.

Stay Ombi runs about $400,000 a year from three floating bungalows in Key Largo, on roughly $40,000 a month in expenses including about $15,000 in labor. It is a small, clean, deliberately simple operation — and the back office is built the same way.

Cloudbeds handles reservations and pushes through to QuickBooks Online. Ramp handles expense management and invoice tracking, already coded carefully on your side. Gusto handles payroll. One operating account. There is no cleanup to do here, and I'm not going to invent one.

What you're buying is the same work, done properly, at a number that fits the size of the business.

Scope of work

Pick it up, keep it clean, hand it off at tax time.

  • Ongoing every month, starting day one. Reconcile the operating account and Ramp activity, verify coding, post Gusto payroll entries, track deferred revenue on advance bookings, and close each month on accrual with a P&L and balance sheet. Plus a monthly review call.
  • A clean transition from your current provider. I take the books as they stand, confirm the opening balances tie out, and pick up from there. No re-doing work you’ve already paid for, and no gap in the middle.
  • In step with your CPA. I own the monthly books; your CPA handles the filings — working from numbers that are already closed and reconciled when they get them.
