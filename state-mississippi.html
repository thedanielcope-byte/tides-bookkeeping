📍 About Mississippi

The Magnolia State's Road to Zero Income Tax.

Mississippi's economy is rooted in agriculture — poultry, cotton, soybeans, and catfish — alongside advanced manufacturing, shipbuilding at Ingalls in Pascagoula, automotive assembly, and a large Gulf Coast tourism and casino industry around Biloxi and Gulfport. Jackson, the capital and largest city, anchors healthcare, government, and services. The state offers some of the lowest costs of doing business in the nation.

The MS tax picture

Mississippi is in the middle of a landmark tax change: lawmakers passed legislation in 2025 to phase out the individual income tax entirely over time. As of 2026 the income tax is a flat 4.00% on taxable income above roughly $10,000, down from prior years, with further reductions scheduled ahead (as of 2026 — confirm current rate and schedule). The state sales tax is 7%, one of the higher base rates in the country, though groceries are now taxed at a reduced rate (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). For small business owners, the shrinking income tax is a genuine tailwind — and clean books make it easy to see the benefit year over year.
