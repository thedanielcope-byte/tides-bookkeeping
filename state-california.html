📍 About California

The World's Fourth-Largest Economy.

California has the largest economy of any U.S. state — big enough to rank among the largest in the world. It's a place of extremes: Silicon Valley tech and biotech, Hollywood entertainment and media, the Central Valley's agriculture, the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach moving a huge share of the nation's trade, plus tourism, wine, and a vast small-business base of contractors, restaurants, and professional services. That diversity means no two California businesses keep books quite the same way.

The CA tax picture

California also has some of the highest taxes in the country, which makes clean books and smart structure matter more here than almost anywhere. State personal income tax is progressive and tops out at 13.3% — the highest top rate in the nation. The statewide base sales-and-use tax is 7.25% (the highest state base rate), and local add-ons push many areas past 10%. Most LLCs owe the $800 minimum annual franchise tax regardless of profit, plus an additional gross-receipts LLC fee once revenue climbs, and S-corporations pay a 1.5% franchise tax. Figures are current as of 2026 — confirm rates before filing. The practical takeaway: in California, disorganized books get expensive fast, and the right entity and records save real money.
