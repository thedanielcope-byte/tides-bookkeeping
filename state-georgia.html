📍 About Georgia

A State Built for Business.

Georgia has the most diverse state economy in the Southeast — anchored by metro Atlanta's corporate and tech base, the Port of Savannah, a booming film industry, and the nation's #1 poultry and agriculture sector.

⛰️
Metro Atlanta
Atlanta · Sandy Springs · Roswell · Marietta · Alpharetta
The economic engine of the Southeast — Fortune 500 headquarters (Delta, Home Depot, UPS, Coca-Cola), the world's busiest airport, a deep fintech and tech sector, and 6+ million people.
🏛️
North & Central Georgia
Athens · Augusta · Macon · Columbus · Gainesville
The Piedmont and mountains — anchored by Augusta (medical & cyber), Columbus (Fort Moore & Aflac), Athens (UGA), Macon, and the fast-growing North Georgia foothills.
🌊
Coastal & South Georgia
Savannah · Brunswick · Valdosta · Albany · Hinesville
The Port of Savannah (2nd-busiest US container port), the Golden Isles tourism economy, Fort Stewart, and South Georgia's agricultural heartland.
🌾
The Pee Dee & Grand Strand
Myrtle Beach · Florence · Conway
The Grand Strand is one of America's busiest tourist corridors — 60 miles of beach driving a massive hospitality economy, while the Pee Dee region is rooted in agriculture and manufacturing.

Georgia's Economy & Business Climate

Georgia is one of the most business-friendly states in America — with no inventory tax, a competitive corporate income tax rate, and a state government that has aggressively recruited global manufacturers for decades. The results speak for themselves: Boeing, Volvo, BMW, Michelin, Mercedes-Benz Vans, and dozens of others have made GA home.

Georgia was the last of the 13 original colonies, founded in 1733 by James Oglethorpe. From an agricultural cotton economy it transformed into the corporate, logistics, and entertainment capital of the Southeast — home to the world's busiest airport and the largest film industry in the nation.

🛳️
Port of Savannah — 2nd-Busiest US Container Port
The Georgia Ports Authority's Savannah terminal is the fastest-growing major port in the country — driving logistics, warehousing, and distribution across the entire state.
✈️
Film & TV — "Hollywood of the South"
Georgia is the top film-production state in the U.S. — Trilith and Tyler Perry Studios anchor a multibillion-dollar industry employing thousands of contractors and support businesses.
🏖️
Corporate HQ & Fintech — Atlanta
Atlanta hosts Delta, Home Depot, UPS, Coca-Cola, and so much payment processing it's called "Transaction Alley" — roughly 70% of U.S. card transactions touch Georgia.
🪖
Military — Fort Moore, Fort Stewart & Robins AFB
Three massive installations — Fort Moore (Columbus), Fort Stewart (Hinesville), and Robins Air Force Base (Warner Robins) — anchor entire regional economies and defense-contractor networks.
🏗️
Volvo Cars USA (Berkeley County)
Volvo's only US manufacturing plant, opened in 2018 — part of a long line of global auto manufacturers choosing GA for its workforce, infrastructure, and business climate.

Key Interstate & Highway Infrastructure

Georgia's highway network is built around Atlanta — the busiest interstate hub in the Southeast — plus the I-95 coastal corridor and I-16 to the Port of Savannah.

I-75 — Georgia's Main Spine
Runs the length of the state — Tennessee through Atlanta to Florida. The primary freight and population corridor.
I-285 — The Atlanta Perimeter
The 64-mile beltway around Atlanta — "ITP vs OTP" defines the metro. Anchors suburban commercial growth.
I-16 — Atlanta's Path to the Port
Macon to Savannah — the critical freight link between central Georgia and the Port of Savannah.
GA-400 — North Atlanta Corridor
The primary route from Atlanta into the affluent northern suburbs (Alpharetta, Cumming) and the North Georgia foothills.
I-20 & I-85 — East & Northeast
I-20 runs Atlanta to Augusta; I-85 runs Atlanta to the northeast — together the spokes of metro Atlanta's hub.
I-20 — Augusta Connector
Connects Columbia to Augusta, GA — the primary route for Aiken County and the western Central Georgia commercial corridor.

GA Landmarks & Cultural Identity

🏛️
Charleston's Historic District
One of the best-preserved antebellum cities in America — a world-class tourism destination and the state's most recognizable cultural export.
🎓
University System of Georgia
The University of Georgia (Athens), Georgia Tech (Atlanta), and Georgia State anchor a higher-ed system of 340,000+ students — fueling the state's tech, research, and professional-services workforce.
🌊
Myrtle Beach — Grand Strand
60 miles of Atlantic coastline, 100+ golf courses, and millions of annual visitors — one of the most economically significant tourism corridors in the Southeast.
