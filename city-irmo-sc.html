📍 About Irmo, SC

The Town Behind the Business.

Irmo isn't just a zip code on the way to Lake Murray — it's a long-established Midlands community with its own neighborhoods, employers, and economy. Knowing how it works is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Irmo was incorporated in 1890 as a railway stop on the Columbia, Newberry & Laurens line. The name itself is a combination — pulled from the surnames of two railway officials, Iredell and Moseley — and that railroad heritage shaped the town's early commercial center along what is now St. Andrews Road.

The completion of the Lake Murray Dam in 1930 turned Irmo's geography into its biggest economic engine. As one of the largest reservoirs in the Southeast filled up just to the west, Irmo became the gateway: where lake residents stopped for groceries, fuel, and dinner on the way home from the water. Decades of suburban growth from Columbia followed, and the Harbison development in the 1980s established the commercial corridor that still drives the town today.

Today Irmo is anchored by the strong Lexington-Richland 5 school district, a deep base of suburban professional services, and steady inflow from the lake economy — all of which means more small business owners who need clean books to grow.
