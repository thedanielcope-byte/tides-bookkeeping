The CO Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and a home-rule sales-tax maze.

Colorado has a flat state income tax (4.4% as of 2026; TABOR can trigger temporary cuts and refunds), and a low 2.9% state sales-tax base. The catch is local: Colorado is a home-rule state, so many cities and districts stack their own sales taxes on top — and the biggest "self-collecting" cities administer and require separate sales-tax filings from the state. That patchwork is where Weld County businesses most often get tripped up, and it's exactly what we keep clean.
