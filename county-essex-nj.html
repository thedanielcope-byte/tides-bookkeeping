The NJ Tax Angle

A high state tax load — and local wrinkles.

New Jersey pairs a graduated income tax topping at 10.75% with the nation's highest corporate rate (9% CBT plus a 2.5% Corporate Transit Fee surtax = 11.5% on net income over $10 million) and a flat 6.625% sales tax. Across Essex County, keeping income, corporate, and sales tax reconciled and filed on time is the real work, and that's exactly what we keep straight.
