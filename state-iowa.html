📍 About Iowa

America's Corn Belt Powerhouse.

Iowa's economy is anchored by agriculture, where it leads the nation in corn, ethanol, pork, and egg production, but it is far more diversified than the fields suggest. Des Moines is one of the country's largest insurance and financial-services hubs, home to Principal Financial and a dense cluster of carriers. Advanced manufacturing (led by ag-equipment maker John Deere), biofuels, wind energy, and food processing round out a steady, broad-based economy.

The IA tax picture

As of 2026, Iowa taxes individual income at a single flat rate of 3.8%, having phased out its old graduated brackets in recent years, though you should confirm the current rate before filing. C-corporations pay a graduated corporate income tax of roughly 5.5% on the first $100,000 and 7.1% above that (as of 2026, confirm current rates). The state sales tax is 6%, and most jurisdictions add a 1% local option sales tax, so many Iowa businesses collect a combined 7%. Groceries and prescription drugs are generally exempt from sales tax.
