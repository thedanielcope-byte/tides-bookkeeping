About the Area

Lee County Is
Southwest Florida's
Fastest-Growing Market.

Lee County is the economic hub of Southwest Florida, anchored by Cape Coral and Fort Myers. One of the fastest-growing counties in the United States, Lee County has attracted a massive influx of residents and businesses driven by Florida's no-income-tax advantage, affordable real estate, and year-round sunshine.

With Florida Gulf Coast University driving workforce development, Southwest Florida International Airport connecting the region to the nation, and a booming construction industry rebuilding after Hurricane Ian, Lee County's small business economy is dynamic and growing rapidly.

History & Economic Identity

Lee County's story is one of explosive growth — from a quiet fishing and agricultural community to one of the fastest-growing metropolitan areas in the United States. Thomas Edison and Henry Ford wintered in Fort Myers in the early 1900s, but the county's population explosion didn't begin until the 1970s and has accelerated dramatically since 2000.

In September 2022, Hurricane Ian made direct landfall in Lee County as a Category 4 storm, causing catastrophic damage across Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel Island, and Cape Coral. The rebuilding effort has generated a massive construction and restoration economy — and renewed focus on resilient building practices and business continuity.

Today, Lee County is rebuilding and growing simultaneously. Cape Coral continues to be one of the fastest-growing cities in America. FGCU is expanding its programs and research. And the county's no-income-tax advantage continues to attract retirees, remote workers, and entrepreneurs from the Northeast and Midwest.
