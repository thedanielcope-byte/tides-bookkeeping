About the Area

Fulton County Is
Georgia's Economic
Powerhouse.

Fulton County is the most populous county in Georgia and home to Atlanta -- the undisputed economic capital of the American Southeast. The county stretches from the affluent northern suburbs of Alpharetta and Roswell through Atlanta's urban core and south to College Park and Fairburn.

With the world's busiest airport, the headquarters of Coca-Cola, Delta Air Lines, Home Depot, and UPS, and a booming tech and film industry, Fulton County generates more economic activity than most US states. That means tens of thousands of small business owners who need clean, reliable books.

History & Economic Identity

Fulton County's economic story is the story of Atlanta itself -- from a railroad terminus founded in 1837 to the economic capital of the American South. The county was created in 1853 and has been the center of Georgia's commerce, government, and culture ever since.

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport -- the world's busiest airport by passenger traffic -- sits in the southern part of the county and drives an enormous logistics, hospitality, and service economy. Midtown and Buckhead house the headquarters of some of America's largest companies, including Coca-Cola, Delta Air Lines, Home Depot, and UPS.

Today, Fulton County blends a massive urban core with affluent northern suburbs like Sandy Springs and Alpharetta, creating an incredibly diverse business landscape. The county's tech sector has exploded, its film industry rivals Hollywood, and its healthcare systems -- Emory Healthcare and Grady Health -- are among the largest in the Southeast.
