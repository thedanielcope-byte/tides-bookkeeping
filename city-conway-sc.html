📍 About Conway, SC

The City Behind the Business.

Conway is more than a zip code — it's one of South Carolina's oldest river towns and the seat of fast-growing Horry County. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1732 as Kingston and later renamed for Gen. Robert Conway, Conway is one of South Carolina's oldest towns. It grew on the banks of the Waccamaw River as a center for the timber, naval-stores, and tobacco trades — and its live-oak-lined historic downtown still reflects that heritage.

Today Conway serves as the inland government, education, and healthcare hub for the booming Myrtle Beach and Grand Strand tourism economy, just 15 miles up US-501. The historic downtown and the Waccamaw Riverwalk anchor the city, while Coastal Carolina University and a steady stream of new residents continue to fuel its growth as the seat of one of the fastest-growing counties in the country.

That growth means more small business owners than ever — and more small business owners who need clean books to grow with confidence.
