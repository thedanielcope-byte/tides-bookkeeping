📍 About Hall County

The Central Nebraska County.

Hall County is the hub of central Nebraska — an agribusiness and food-processing center anchored by Grand Island, home to the Nebraska State Fair and a major beef-processing and rail economy.

We serve businesses across the county — Grand Island, and the smaller communities in between.

The NE Tax Angle

An income tax being cut — and a 5.5% sales tax with local add-ons.

Nebraska charges a 5.5% state sales tax that Hall County cities can top with up to 2% in local option (about 7% combined in the metros), and its individual income tax is being cut toward a flat 3.99% by 2027. We keep your income, sales, and local-option records clean.
