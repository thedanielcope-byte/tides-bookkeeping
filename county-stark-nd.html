📍 About Stark County

The Southwest County.

Stark County sits on the southern edge of the Bakken in southwestern North Dakota — an energy, ranching, and agricultural economy anchored by Dickinson and Dickinson State University.

We serve businesses across the county — Dickinson, and the smaller communities in between.

The ND Tax Angle

A rock-bottom income tax — and a 5% sales tax with local add-ons.

North Dakota levies one of the nation's lowest income taxes — a top rate of just 2.5% — plus a 5% state sales tax that Stark County cities top with local option taxes (roughly 6-8.5% combined). We keep your income, sales, and local-option records clean.
