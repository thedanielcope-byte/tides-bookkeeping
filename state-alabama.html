📍 About Alabama

From Rocket City to the Auto Belt.

Alabama has shifted from its cotton-and-steel past into advanced manufacturing. Huntsville, known as Rocket City, anchors a major aerospace and defense cluster around NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center and Redstone Arsenal, while the state has become a Southern auto-manufacturing powerhouse with plants for Mercedes-Benz, Honda, Hyundai, and the Mazda-Toyota joint venture. Birmingham remains a banking, healthcare, and biotech center, and the Port of Mobile drives shipbuilding and international trade.

The AL tax picture

Alabama pairs a modest income tax with one of the heaviest sales-tax burdens in the country. The individual income tax is graduated from 2% to a top rate of 5%, but that top rate kicks in at very low income (above $3,000 for single filers), so most earners effectively pay 5% (as of 2026 — confirm the current rate before filing). The state sales-tax base is 4%, but city and county add-ons push the combined rate to an average near 9.5% — among the highest in the nation — with some jurisdictions exceeding 11%. Alabama also levies a business privilege tax on entities doing business in the state. Sales-tax compliance is the real headache here, since rates and rules vary sharply by locality.
