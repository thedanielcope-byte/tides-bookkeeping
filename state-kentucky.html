📍 About Kentucky

The Bourbon and Bluegrass Powerhouse.

Kentucky blends heritage industries with heavy manufacturing and logistics. It produces roughly 95% of the world's bourbon and is the heart of the Thoroughbred horse industry, while also hosting major auto plants (Toyota in Georgetown and Ford in Louisville) and one of the world's largest air-cargo hubs, UPS Worldport in Louisville. Agriculture, coal, and healthcare fill out a diverse economic base.

The KY tax picture

Kentucky levies a flat individual income tax, scheduled to be 3.5% for 2026 (down from 4.0% in prior years), though you should confirm the current rate before filing. The state sales tax is 6%, and Kentucky has no local sales taxes. The signature quirk is local and separate from state tax: most Kentucky cities and counties impose occupational license taxes on wages and/or net business profits (for example, in Louisville and Lexington), administered locally. Pass-through profits flow to owners' individual returns, while C-corporations pay Kentucky corporate income tax.
