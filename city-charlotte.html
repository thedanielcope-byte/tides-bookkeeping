📍 About Charlotte, NC

The City Behind the Business.

Charlotte is more than a zip code — it's one of North Carolina's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1768 and named after Queen Charlotte of Mecklenburg-Strelitz, Charlotte grew from a small courthouse town into the financial capital of the Southeast. The discovery of gold at Reed Gold Mine in 1799 established the city as America's first gold rush town, and the US Mint operated here from 1837 to 1913.

The modern transformation began when NCNB (now Bank of America) and First Union (now Wells Fargo/Truist) grew into national banking powerhouses in the 1980s and '90s, making Charlotte the second-largest banking center in the US after New York. Today, the city's economy has diversified well beyond finance — with major growth in tech, healthcare, energy, and professional services.

That growth means more small business owners than ever — and more small business owners who need clean books to grow with confidence.
