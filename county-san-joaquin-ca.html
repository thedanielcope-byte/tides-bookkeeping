📍 About San Joaquin County

The Delta County.

San Joaquin County sits at the head of the California Delta — an agriculture, distribution, and food-processing economy with one of the state's few inland seaports, anchored by Stockton.

We serve businesses across the county — Stockton, and the smaller communities in between.

The CA Tax Angle

The nation's highest income tax — and a 7.25%+ sales tax.

California levies the nation's highest personal income tax (graduated to 13.3%) and a 7.25% base sales tax that district taxes push past 10% in parts of San Joaquin County, and every LLC and corporation owes the $800 minimum annual franchise tax to the Franchise Tax Board. We keep your income, sales, and franchise-tax records clean.
