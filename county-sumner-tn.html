About the Area

Sumner County Is
North Nashville's
Fastest-Growing Suburb.

Sumner County sits just northeast of Nashville and has become one of Middle Tennessee's fastest-growing counties. Anchored by the cities of Hendersonville and Gallatin, it offers a mix of suburban residential growth, lakeside tourism along Old Hickory Lake, and a diversified manufacturing base that includes companies like Gap Inc. and Servpro's national headquarters.

The county's proximity to Nashville makes it an attractive location for professionals who want suburban living with easy access to the city. That's driving rapid growth in healthcare, retail, home construction, and professional services — all of which need clean, reliable bookkeeping.

Key Employers & Economic Drivers

🏢
Servpro National Headquarters
The national headquarters of Servpro franchise system is in Gallatin — one of the largest franchise operations in the country and a major Sumner County employer.
🏭
Gap Inc. Distribution Center
Gap's massive Gallatin distribution center is one of the county's largest employers — driving logistics, warehousing, and supply chain businesses.
🌊
Old Hickory Lake
The lake drives a significant tourism, marina, and recreation economy in Hendersonville and Gallatin — boat rentals, fishing guides, lakeside restaurants, and vacation rentals.
🎵
Hendersonville Music Heritage
Hendersonville has deep country music roots — Johnny Cash and Conway Twitty called it home. The music heritage continues to drive tourism and cultural businesses.

Key Corridors & Communities

Indian Lake / Hendersonville
Hendersonville's main commercial hub — retail, restaurants, healthcare, and professional services centered around Indian Lake Boulevard.
Nashville Pike / Gallatin
The commercial corridor connecting Gallatin to Hendersonville — auto dealers, retailers, and professional offices along US-31E.
Downtown Gallatin / The Square
The county seat's revitalized historic square with boutiques, restaurants, law firms, and professional services.
Long Hollow Pike / Goodlettsville
The corridor connecting Goodlettsville to Hendersonville — growing commercial district with restaurants, retail, and Servpro's HQ.
I-65 / White House
Northern Sumner County's interstate corridor with growing retail, restaurants, and service businesses at the I-65 interchange.
Vietnam Veterans Blvd
Hendersonville's major east-west route with healthcare offices, professional services, and commercial development.
Portland / TN-52
Northern Sumner County's agricultural and commercial corridor with local retailers, feed stores, and service businesses.
Lakeside / Old Hickory Lake
The lake-adjacent corridor with marinas, restaurants, boat services, and vacation rental businesses.

Major Routes Across the County

🛣️
US-31E / Nashville Pike
The primary north-south route through Sumner County connecting Hendersonville, Gallatin, Portland, and points north.
🛣️
I-65 — Interstate Corridor
Runs through the western edge of Sumner County at White House and Goodlettsville — connecting to Nashville and Kentucky.
🛣️
SR-386 / Vietnam Veterans Blvd
The east-west expressway connecting Hendersonville directly to I-65 — a key commuter and commercial route.
