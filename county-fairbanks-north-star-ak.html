About the Area

The Interior's Hub -
University, Bases,
Gold and Aurora.

The Fairbanks North Star Borough is the economic center of Interior Alaska and the second-largest borough population in the state. It contains the City of Fairbanks, the City of North Pole, and a wide ring of unincorporated communities including Ester, Fox, Salcha, and Two Rivers.

Four things drive the borough economy: the University of Alaska Fairbanks, the military (Fort Wainwright and nearby Eielson Air Force Base), gold mining, and tourism - which here means aurora season, a winter business, not a summer one. Fairbanks is also the staging point for the Dalton Highway and the North Slope, so a lot of oilfield logistics runs through town.

It is a borough of small, tough, seasonal businesses: trades with a short build window, tour operators whose peak is in January, mining service companies, and the restaurants and shops that serve a garrison-and-campus town.

History & Economic Identity

Fairbanks was founded on an accident: in 1901 a trader named E.T. Barnette got stranded on the Chena River, and a year later gold was found nearby. The Fairbanks gold rush built the town, the Alaska Railroad connected it, and World War II and the Cold War planted the military that has anchored it ever since.

The Trans-Alaska Pipeline changed everything again in the 1970s. Fairbanks was the construction staging point for the pipeline's northern half, and it remains the last real city before the Dalton Highway runs 414 miles to Deadhorse and the North Slope oil fields. If you are trucking to the Slope, your yard is probably in Fairbanks.

Today the borough runs on a four-legged stool - UAF (the University of Alaska's flagship research campus, home of the Geophysical Institute), the military (Fort Wainwright in town and Eielson AFB just south, which received the Air Force's F-35 mission), gold (the Fort Knox mine northeast of town is one of the largest producers in Alaska), and aurora tourism, which fills hotels in the dead of winter with visitors from Japan, Korea, and China. That last one is why a Fairbanks tour operator's cash flow chart looks upside down compared to the rest of the country.

🎓
University of Alaska Fairbanks
The UA system's flagship research campus and one of the borough's largest employers. UAF's Geophysical Institute and Arctic research programs pull in federal grant money and support a cluster of research-adjacent contractors.
🎖️
Fort Wainwright & Eielson AFB
Fort Wainwright sits inside Fairbanks; Eielson AFB is just down the Richardson Highway and hosts F-35 squadrons. Together they drive construction, defense subcontracting, housing, and a large steady payroll.
⛏️
Fort Knox Gold Mine
Northeast of Fairbanks on the Steese Highway - one of Alaska's biggest gold producers, and the anchor of a mining-services economy of equipment dealers, haulers, welders, and camp caterers.
🌌
Aurora Tourism
Fairbanks sits under the auroral oval, which makes it one of the best places on earth to see the northern lights. That produces a genuinely inverted season: hotels, tour vans, and photography guides are busiest from September through March.

💰 The Alaska Tax Angle

No State Sales Tax.
But Read This Anyway.

Alaska has no state sales tax and no state personal income tax — which is exactly why so many business owners get Alaska tax wrong. More than 100 local jurisdictions levy their own sales tax, and the rules change borough by borough and city by city.

Fairbanks North Star Borough: The Numbers

Alaska has no state sales tax and no state personal income tax - and the Fairbanks North Star Borough repealed its own borough sales tax back in 1987. But there is one important exception inside the borough, and a lot of Fairbanks businesses get it wrong.

Alaska Personal Income Tax None

What It Means for Your Books

⚠️
North Pole is the exception - and it is the one people miss
The Fairbanks North Star Borough levies no general sales tax and neither does the City of Fairbanks. But the City of North Pole does: a 5.5% general sales tax with a cap on the taxable amount per sale (roughly $16.50 maximum collected per transaction). If you have a storefront in North Pole, or you deliver and install there, that is a real filing obligation your Fairbanks competitors do not have. Rates are as of 2026 - confirm with the City of North Pole.
🍺
Excise taxes still exist without a general sales tax
The borough and city levy taxes on tobacco, alcohol, marijuana, and hotel room rentals even though general retail is untaxed. If you run a bar, a hotel, an aurora lodge, or a dispensary, you have a local filing - and the bed tax in particular catches a lot of new short-term rental operators off guard.
🌐
ARSSTC applies to what you ship out, not what you sell here
No sales tax in Fairbanks does not mean no sales tax on what you ship to Juneau, Sitka, Kodiak, or Bethel. The Alaska Remote Sellers Sales Tax Commission (ARSSTC) handles single-point registration and filing. Threshold: $100,000 in gross statewide sales in the current or prior calendar year. The 200-transaction test was removed effective January 1, 2025.
