📍 About Wyoming

No Income Tax in the Cowboy State.

Wyoming's economy runs on energy and minerals - it is a top U.S. producer of coal, natural gas, and trona (soda ash) - supplemented by federal mineral royalties that fund much of state government. Tourism is the other pillar, drawing millions of visitors to Yellowstone and Grand Teton National Parks, alongside ranching and agriculture. The state's low taxes and light regulation have also made it a popular home for LLCs and small businesses.

The WY tax picture

Wyoming is one of the most tax-friendly states in the country: there is no personal income tax and no corporate income tax at all. The state funds itself largely through severance taxes on coal, oil, natural gas, and trona, plus federal mineral royalties, which keeps the burden off individuals and most businesses. Sales tax is low - a 4% state rate plus up to 2% in local options (about 5.4% on average, as of 2026 - confirm current rate). With no franchise tax and modest filing fees, Wyoming is a popular state for forming LLCs and holding companies.
