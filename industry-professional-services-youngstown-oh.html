Built for Youngstown Firms

Built for Every Type of
Youngstown Firms.

Youngstown's professional-services bench runs deep — and the books behind these firms look nothing like a retail P&L. Here's how Tides fits the partners, principals, and owners building practices across Mahoning County.

The the Mahoning Valley pro-services density is substantial. Engineering and consulting firms here support the corporate and manufacturing corridors anchored by GM's Lordstown corridor, Vallourec, and Youngstown State University. Alongside them, law firms handle corporate matters, IP work, and a flood of real estate and construction law driven by the building boom.

Downtown and the surrounding corridors are home to a dense mix of marketing, branding, PR, and creative agencies serving regional and national clients, with architects and design studios filling out the picture. For all of them, payroll and owner draws run headfirst into Ohio's municipal income tax: Youngstown runs its own municipal income tax while the surrounding Mahoning Valley communities file through RITA, so multi-jurisdiction withholding is a constant for any Valley business with a mobile workforce. And once a growing firm crosses Ohio's $3 million Commercial Activity Tax (CAT) gross-receipts threshold, that becomes one more filing to stay ahead of.

The bookkeeping reality is the same across the board: revenue ties to billable hours, retainers, or project milestones — not invoice dates. We integrate with Clio, Harvest, Toggl, and similar time systems, build a chart of accounts that throws off real project and client P&L, handle IOLTA reconciliation for law firms, keep multi-city municipal withholding straight, and clean up partner distributions, equity draws, and billable utilization so principals know exactly what each engagement earns.
