📍 About Indiana

The Crossroads of America.

Indiana sits at the center of the country's manufacturing and logistics network — with a low cost of living, a flat 3.05% state income tax, and an economy anchored by automotive manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, agriculture, and one of the most famous sporting events on Earth.

🏙️
Central Indiana
Indianapolis · Carmel · Fishers · Noblesville · Greenwood
The state capital and economic hub — home to Eli Lilly, Salesforce Tower, the Indianapolis Motor Speedway, one of the largest convention centers in the country, and the booming Hamilton County suburbs of Carmel and Fishers consistently ranked among the best places to live in America.
🏭
Northeast Indiana
Fort Wayne · Warsaw · Auburn · Huntington
Indiana's second-largest city and a deep manufacturing hub — Fort Wayne anchors a region that includes Warsaw, the "Orthopedic Capital of the World" (Zimmer Biomet, DePuy Synthes), and a strong defense and automotive supplier base.
🏖️
Northwest Indiana
Gary · Hammond · Crown Point · Valparaiso · Michigan City
Known locally as "The Region" — the Chicago commuter belt on the Lake Michigan shore. Indiana Dunes National Park, the US Steel and ArcelorMittal mills, and a logistics corridor that connects the Port of Indiana-Burns Harbor to the rest of the state.
🌾
Southern Indiana
Evansville · Bloomington · New Albany · Jeffersonville
Indiana University in Bloomington, the Toyota plant in Princeton, the Louisville metro spillover in Clark and Floyd counties, and Evansville — the largest city on the Ohio River between Louisville and the Mississippi.

Indiana's Economy & Business Climate

Indiana is consistently ranked among the most business-friendly states in the country — with the lowest business-tax burden in the Midwest, a flat 3.05% individual income tax rate, no inventory tax, and a regulatory environment that has attracted global automakers, pharmaceutical giants, and logistics operations for decades.

Admitted as the 19th state in 1816, Indiana earned its motto "The Crossroads of America" honestly — more interstate highways pass through Indiana than any other state, making it one of the most connected logistics hubs in the nation. That infrastructure advantage, combined with a deep manufacturing workforce and low cost of living, continues to drive growth.

💊
Eli Lilly & Co. — Indianapolis
One of the world's largest pharmaceutical companies, headquartered in Indianapolis since 1876. Eli Lilly is the largest private employer in Indiana and a major anchor of the state's life sciences corridor.
🚗
Automotive Manufacturing Powerhouse
Subaru's only US plant is in Lafayette. Toyota builds trucks in Princeton. Honda assembles vehicles in Greensburg. GM builds full-size trucks in Fort Wayne. Indiana produces more vehicles per capita than nearly any other state.
🏎️
Indianapolis Motor Speedway — The Brickyard
Home of the Indianapolis 500, the world's largest single-day sporting event. The Speedway and its surrounding motorsports industry generate billions in economic impact and define Indianapolis on the global stage.
🌽
Agriculture — Top 10 Producer
Indiana ranks in the top 5 nationally for corn and soybean production, with a massive hog farming sector. Agribusiness drives thousands of small businesses across rural Indiana.
🦴
Warsaw — Orthopedic Capital of the World
Warsaw, Indiana is home to Zimmer Biomet, DePuy Synthes, and a cluster of orthopedic device manufacturers that produce the majority of the world's orthopedic implants — a uniquely Indiana industry cluster.

Key Interstate & Highway Infrastructure

Indiana's motto is "The Crossroads of America" for a reason — more major interstates intersect in Indiana than in any other state, making it one of the top logistics and distribution hubs in the country.

I-65 — The Indiana Spine
Gary to Indianapolis to Louisville — the primary north-south artery connecting Chicago, the Indy metro, and Kentucky.
I-70 — East-West Corridor
Terre Haute through Indianapolis to Richmond — the major east-west freight and commuter route crossing the heart of the state.
I-69 — Indy to Fort Wayne & Beyond
Connects Indianapolis northeast to Fort Wayne and is being extended south to Evansville — a transformative infrastructure project for southern Indiana.
I-465 — The Indianapolis Loop
One of the longest urban beltways in the US — encircling Indianapolis and connecting every major interstate that enters the state.
I-74 — Indy to Cincinnati
Connects Indianapolis southeast to the Cincinnati metro — the primary corridor for businesses straddling the Indiana-Ohio border.
US-31 — Indy to South Bend
Being upgraded to full freeway — connects Indianapolis north through Kokomo and Peru to South Bend and the Notre Dame corridor.

IN Landmarks & Cultural Identity

🏁
Indianapolis Motor Speedway
The Brickyard — home of the Indy 500 since 1911, the largest permanent sports venue in the world (capacity 250,000+), and the anchor of Indiana's global motorsports identity.
🏖️
Indiana Dunes National Park
Fifteen miles of Lake Michigan shoreline — dunes, prairies, wetlands, and old-growth forests. Indiana's only national park and a growing eco-tourism destination on the Northwest Indiana coast.
🏈
University of Notre Dame — South Bend
One of the most recognizable universities in the country — Notre Dame's campus, football tradition, and research economy anchor the South Bend–Mishawaka metro and draw visitors from around the world.
