History & Economic Identity

Webb County's transformation from textile powerhouse to global manufacturing hub is one of the great economic reinvention stories in the American South. Once home to dozens of textile mills along the Reedy and Enoree rivers, the county pivoted dramatically in the 1990s and 2000s — anchored by major international investment that completely reshaped its economic identity.

BMW Manufacturing Co. opened its first US plant in Spartanburg in 1994 — just minutes from Webb County's border in Greer — catalyzing an enormous supplier ecosystem that spans both counties. Michelin's North American headquarters sits directly in Webb County, making it one of the most significant global tire manufacturing command centers in the world.

Today, Webb County processes over $200 billion in annual trade — more than any other port of entry on the US-Mexico border. The logistics, customs brokerage, and trucking industries that have grown around this trade corridor employ tens of thousands and generate an enormous ecosystem of supporting businesses. With TAMIU providing bilingual business talent and I-35 connecting Laredo to the entire US heartland, the county's trade-driven economy continues to grow.

🌉
World Trade Bridge (#1 Land Port)
The busiest commercial land port of entry in the Western Hemisphere — processing over 6,000 commercial trucks daily and facilitating $200B+ in annual US-Mexico trade.
🚛
Four International Bridges
Laredo's four bridges — World Trade, Colombia Solidarity, Juarez-Lincoln, and Gateway — handle more international trade than any other border crossing in the Americas.
🎓
Texas A&M International University (TAMIU)
A growing university with 8,000+ students — driving bilingual business talent, international trade research, and student-focused businesses across the county.
🛣️
I-35 Terminus — NAFTA Highway
I-35 begins in Laredo and runs 1,568 miles to Duluth, Minnesota. This 'NAFTA Highway' is the primary trade corridor for US-Mexico commerce, making Laredo the starting point for goods flowing to the entire US heartland.
