📍 About Savannah, GA

The City Behind the Business.

Savannah is more than a zip code — it's one of Georgia's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded on February 12, 1733, by General James Oglethorpe, Savannah was the first settlement in the colony of Georgia — the 13th and final American colony. Oglethorpe laid out the city in a revolutionary grid of public squares, creating America's first planned city. Twenty-two of the original 24 squares survive today, forming the largest National Historic Landmark District in the United States.

The 20th century brought industrial diversification and a cultural renaissance: the founding of the Historic Savannah Foundation in 1955 saved over 350 buildings, SCAD (founded 1978) revitalized dozens of historic structures, and John Berendt's "Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil" (1994) transformed Savannah into a global tourism destination. The Georgia Ports Authority, established in 1945, set the stage for Savannah's rise as a major logistics hub.

Today that blend of preservation and progress continues — with more small business owners than ever, and more who need clean books to grow with confidence.
