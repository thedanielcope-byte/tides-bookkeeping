The ME Tax Angle

A graduated income tax — and a tiered, no-local sales tax.

Maine has no local sales tax, so the rate is the same across Hancock County — but it's tiered: 5.5% general, 8% on prepared meals, 9% on lodging, and 10% on short-term auto rentals, on top of a graduated income tax topping at 7.15%. For the county's restaurants, inns, and seasonal businesses, getting the right rate on every sale is the work, and that's what we keep clean.
