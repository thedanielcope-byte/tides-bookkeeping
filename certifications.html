Why these four

Each one maps to a system your books actually run on. QuickBooks Online is where most of our clients keep their books. Gusto and QuickBooks Workforce cover payroll. Puzzle is the ledger we use for startups that live on Stripe and modern banks. A2X is how ecommerce sellers get marketplace payouts to match their bank deposits.

A software certification means someone was trained on the system by the company that makes it, and then passed that company's test. It doesn't replace experience, and it isn't a license. It does tell you your bookkeeper won't be learning the platform on your books.

What these are not

None of these is a CPA license, and Tides isn't a CPA firm. We keep your books clean and reconciled every month, and we hand your CPA a tax-ready file. Your CPA prepares and signs the return. If you're not sure where one job ends and the other starts, bookkeeper vs. CPA vs. accountant lays it out.

Certifications FAQ

Who holds these certifications?

Daniel Cope, Tides' lead bookkeeper. He's the person who does the work on every client's books, so the certifications apply to your account directly, not to a sales team.

How can I verify them?

The QuickBooks certification shows on Daniel's Intuit Find a ProAdvisor listing. The Puzzle and A2X certificates carry a certificate ID, and all three certificates are shown on their pages here. We'll send a copy of any of them on request.

Are you a CPA?

No. Tides is a bookkeeping firm. We do the monthly bookkeeping, reconciliation, reporting and payroll support, and we work alongside your CPA, who handles tax returns and tax advice.

Do software certifications expire?

Some do. Intuit requires QuickBooks ProAdvisors to recertify every year, which is why the Intuit directory is the best place to check someone's current status. We keep this page to what's current.

Do you only work in these platforms?

Most clients are on QuickBooks Online, with Gusto or QuickBooks for payroll. We also keep books in Puzzle and Restaurant365, and use A2X for ecommerce sellers. If you're on something else, ask. We'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
