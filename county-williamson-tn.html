About the Area

Williamson County Is
Tennessee's Wealthiest
& Fastest-Growing County.

Williamson County is consistently ranked as one of the wealthiest and fastest-growing counties in Tennessee and the entire Southeast. Anchored by the city of Franklin and the community of Brentwood, it's home to major corporate headquarters including Nissan North America, Mitsubishi Motors, Mars Petcare, and Community Health Systems.

Franklin's charming downtown, Cool Springs' corporate corridor, and Brentwood's affluent professional community create a unique economic mix. The county attracts high-income professionals, corporate relocations, and entrepreneurs seeking a high quality of life with proximity to Nashville. That means sophisticated small businesses that demand professional-grade bookkeeping.

Key Employers & Economic Drivers

🏢
Cool Springs Corporate Corridor
Franklin's massive corporate and retail district along I-65 — home to Nissan North America, Community Health Systems, Mars Petcare, and dozens of corporate headquarters.
🏛️
Historic Downtown Franklin
One of America's most charming main streets — packed with boutiques, restaurants, galleries, and professional services in beautifully preserved historic buildings.
🏥
Community Health Systems (HQ)
One of the largest hospital operators in the US is headquartered in Franklin — driving healthcare management, consulting, and professional services across the county.
🚗
Nissan North America (HQ)
Nissan's North American headquarters in Cool Springs employs thousands and attracts auto industry suppliers, consultants, and professional service firms.

Key Corridors & Communities

Downtown Franklin / Main Street
Franklin's historic core — boutiques, restaurants, law firms, real estate agencies, and professional services in a walkable downtown district.
Cool Springs / Mallory Lane
The corporate and retail powerhouse — Fortune 500 headquarters, professional offices, shopping centers, and restaurants along I-65 at Exits 68-69.
Brentwood / Maryland Way
Corporate offices, healthcare practices, financial advisors, and professional services in Williamson County's most affluent corridor.
Spring Hill / Port Royal
The fast-growing southern gateway — new retail centers, healthcare facilities, and professional offices serving a booming residential population.
Nolensville Road / 31A
Eastern Williamson County's emerging commercial corridor with new restaurants, retail, and professional services.
Fairview / Highway 100
Western Williamson County's rural-suburban corridor with agricultural businesses, contractors, and local service providers.
Arrington / College Grove
Southern rural communities known for vineyards, event venues, equestrian businesses, and agri-tourism.
I-65 / Goose Creek Bypass
The primary north-south transportation spine connecting Brentwood, Franklin, and Spring Hill.

Major Routes Across the County

🛣️
I-65 — Primary North-South Corridor
Runs through the heart of Williamson County connecting Nashville to Cool Springs, Franklin, and Spring Hill — the county's economic lifeline.
🛣️
US-431 / Hillsboro Road
Connects Franklin and Brentwood to Nashville's Green Hills — a corridor of professional services, healthcare, and upscale residential areas.
🛣️
SR-840 — Outer Loop
The southern beltway connecting Williamson County to Rutherford, Maury, and Dickson counties without going through Nashville.
