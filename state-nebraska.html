📍 About Nebraska

Home of Berkshire Hathaway.

Nebraska's economy is anchored by agriculture — it's a national leader in cattle, corn, and soybeans — along with food processing, insurance and financial services, freight rail and transportation, and telecommunications. Omaha, the largest city, is a corporate hub and headquarters to Berkshire Hathaway, Union Pacific, and Mutual of Omaha, while Lincoln, the capital, adds government, education, and a growing tech scene. The state's low unemployment and central location support a stable small-business climate.

The NE tax picture

Nebraska is aggressively cutting income taxes. Effective January 1, 2026, the top individual income tax rate dropped to 4.55%, part of a multi-year plan aiming for 3.99% (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). The state sales tax is 5.5%, with local options bringing the average combined rate to about 7% (as of 2026 — confirm current rate), and Social Security benefits are now fully exempt from state tax. For pass-through business owners, the falling top rate directly lowers the tax on business profits. Accurate monthly books help you capture the benefit and plan estimated payments.
