What the certification covers

Intuit's payroll certification is five courses and an exam, built around running payroll inside QuickBooks Online correctly and keeping it tied out to the books. The curriculum covers:

  • Setup: federal and state payroll tax accounts, pay schedules, pay types and direct deposit.
  • Paychecks: wage types, gross-to-net calculations, scheduled and unscheduled payroll, final paychecks for terminated employees, and Auto Payroll.
  • Deductions and benefits: health and disability benefits, retirement plans, paid and unpaid time off, and taxable versus non-taxable fringe benefits.
  • Taxes and compliance: payroll taxes and workers' comp, depositing and filing payroll taxes, liability payments, and handling tax penalties.
  • Reporting and year-end: payroll reports, reconciling payroll, customizing payroll expense accounts, and W-2s.

Intuit renamed its payroll line QuickBooks Workforce in 2026, so the certification now shows under that name on the directory.

What it means for your books

Payroll is one of the places small-business books go quietly wrong. Wages land in one lump expense account, employer taxes get mixed in with them, and the payroll liability accounts carry balances nobody can explain. We set payroll up so wages, employer taxes and deductions each post where they belong, and we reconcile the liability accounts back to zero every cycle.

Day to day, most of our clients keep their books in QuickBooks Online: bank feeds and rules, monthly reconciliations, classes and locations for multi-location businesses, projects for job costing, and a clean chart of accounts your CPA can work with. If you're setting QuickBooks up for the first time, or fixing a file someone else built, that's our software setup and cleanup service.

How to check any ProAdvisor

A badge on a website is a claim. The Intuit directory is the proof. Search the person's name at Intuit's Find a ProAdvisor site and look at the "Certified expert in" section: it only shows certifications that are current. Intuit requires ProAdvisors to recertify every year, so a certification from two years ago may not still count. We wrote a full guide on what a QuickBooks ProAdvisor is and how to verify one.

QuickBooks ProAdvisor FAQ

What is a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor?

An accountant or bookkeeper in Intuit's ProAdvisor program who has passed at least one of Intuit's certification exams. Anyone can join the program. Only those who pass an exam are certified, and the certification has to be renewed every year.

Which QuickBooks certification does Daniel hold?

QuickBooks Workforce, Intuit's payroll certification (it was called QuickBooks Payroll until Intuit renamed the product line). It's listed on his Find a ProAdvisor profile.

Is a ProAdvisor the same as a CPA?

No. A ProAdvisor certification shows training and testing in QuickBooks. A CPA is a state license to practice public accounting. We handle the bookkeeping and payroll; your CPA handles the tax return.

Do you work inside my existing QuickBooks file?

Yes. We'll work in your current QuickBooks Online file, clean it up if it needs it, or set up a new one. You keep ownership of the file either way.
