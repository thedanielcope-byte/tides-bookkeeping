The PA Tax Angle

A flat state income tax — and a local-tax maze.

Pennsylvania's signature complexity isn't the flat 3.07% state income tax — it's the local layer. Under Act 32, most employers across Lancaster County must withhold a local Earned Income Tax (EIT, commonly around 1% but set by each municipality and school district) plus a Local Services Tax (up to $52 a year per employee), and remit them to a single regional collector by PSD code. We keep your payroll withholding mapped to the right jurisdictions so your local filings stay accurate.
