📍 About Oktibbeha County

The Mississippi State County.

Oktibbeha County is home to Mississippi State University, the largest university in the state &mdash; a college-town economy anchored by the university, the Thad Cochran Research Park, and OCH Regional Medical Center, led by Starkville.

We serve businesses across the county — Starkville, and the smaller communities in between.

The MS Tax Angle

A high state sales tax — and an income tax on the way out.

Mississippi charges a high 7% state sales tax — one of the highest state rates in the country — but unlike most states it adds almost no local sales tax, so Oktibbeha County businesses generally charge a flat 7% (groceries at a reduced 5%). Layer on a flat income tax, now 4% and legislated under the Build Up Mississippi Act to fall toward full elimination. We keep your sales tax and income-tax picture clean.
