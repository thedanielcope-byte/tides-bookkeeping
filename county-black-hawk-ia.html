The Cedar Valley County.

Black Hawk County anchors the Cedar Valley on the Cedar River — a manufacturing economy built around John Deere and food processing, paired with the University of Northern Iowa and a growing technology base, anchored by Waterloo and Cedar Falls.

We serve businesses across the county — Waterloo, Cedar Falls, and the smaller communities in between.

The IA Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and a 6% sales tax with local add-ons.

Iowa now levies a flat 3.8% individual income tax after phasing out its graduated brackets, plus a 6% state sales tax that most Black Hawk County jurisdictions top with a 1% local-option tax (about 7% combined). The corporate income-tax rate is falling toward a flat 5.5% as well. We keep your income, sales, and payroll records clean.
