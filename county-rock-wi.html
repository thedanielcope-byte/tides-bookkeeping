📍 About Rock County

Southern Wisconsin's Comeback County.

Rock County anchors southern Wisconsin on the Rock River &mdash; a diversified manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare economy that has reinvented itself since the closure of the Janesville GM plant, led by Janesville and Beloit.

We serve businesses across the county — Janesville, and the smaller communities in between.

The WI Tax Angle

A graduated income tax — and the M&A Credit.

Wisconsin pairs a graduated income tax (top rate 7.65%) with a 5% state sales tax and a 0.5% Rock County tax, and its signature Manufacturing & Agriculture Credit nearly eliminates state income tax on qualified manufacturing and farm-production income. We keep your income and sales tax clean and your books ready for the credit.
