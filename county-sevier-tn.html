About the Area

Sevier County Is
Tennessee's Tourism
Capital.

Sevier County is Tennessee's undisputed tourism powerhouse — home to Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg, and the main entrance to Great Smoky Mountains National Park (America's most visited national park with 12+ million annual visitors). Dollywood, Ripley's attractions, and hundreds of cabin rental operations make Sevier County one of the most tourism-dependent economies in America.

Beyond the headline attractions, Sevier County's economy includes thousands of vacation rental operators, restaurants, entertainment venues, retail shops, and service businesses that keep the tourism machine running. From cabin management companies to pancake houses and zip-line operators, every business type in Sevier County faces unique seasonal bookkeeping challenges.

Key Employers & Economic Drivers

🎢
Dollywood Theme Park & Resort
Dolly Parton's flagship theme park in Pigeon Forge is Sevier County's largest employer and the region's #1 attraction — driving thousands of seasonal jobs and support businesses.
🏔️
Great Smoky Mountains National Park
America's most visited national park with 12+ million annual visitors — the economic engine driving Sevier County's entire tourism, hospitality, and recreation economy.
🏠
Cabin Rental Industry
Sevier County has one of the densest cabin rental markets in America — thousands of property management companies, cleaning services, and maintenance businesses supporting the vacation rental ecosystem.
🎭
Pigeon Forge Entertainment District
The Parkway in Pigeon Forge features dozens of dinner theaters, attractions, and entertainment venues — each generating complex seasonal revenue and high transaction volumes.

Key Corridors & Communities

The Parkway / Pigeon Forge
Sevier County's main tourist strip — miles of restaurants, attractions, theaters, and retail along US-441 (the Parkway) through Pigeon Forge.
Downtown Gatlinburg / Ski Mountain
A walkable downtown with hundreds of shops, restaurants, and attractions plus Ober Mountain Ski Resort and surrounding cabin communities.
Dollywood Lane / Music Road
The Dollywood corridor with the theme park, resort hotels, dinner theaters, and supporting businesses.
Sevierville / Winfield Dunn Pkwy
The commercial gateway from I-40 to the tourist corridor — growing retail, restaurants, and outlet shopping.
Wears Valley Road
The scenic alternative route between Pigeon Forge and Townsend — cabin rentals, artisan shops, and mountain restaurants.
US-441 / Newfound Gap Road
The route through the national park connecting Gatlinburg to Cherokee, NC — seasonal outdoor outfitters and activity providers.
Governor's Crossing
Sevierville's growing commercial district with retail, restaurants, and entertainment near the I-40 exit.
Kodak / Exit 407
The I-40 interchange community with outlet malls, restaurants, and the primary gateway to Sevier County from Knoxville.

Major Routes Across the County

🛣️
US-441 (The Parkway)
The main north-south route through Sevier County connecting Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, and Gatlinburg — the economic spine of the county's tourism economy.
🛣️
I-40 — Interstate Access (Exit 407)
Connects Sevier County to Knoxville and the national highway system — the gateway for millions of tourists arriving from across the country.
🛣️
Newfound Gap Road
The scenic route through Great Smoky Mountains National Park — seasonal road with tourism impact on both the Tennessee and North Carolina sides.
