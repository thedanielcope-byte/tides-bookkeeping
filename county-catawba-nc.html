About the Area

Catawba County Is
NC's Manufacturing
Powerhouse.

Catawba County sits along the I-40 corridor in western North Carolina's foothills, anchored by the city of Hickory. Once known worldwide as the "Furniture Capital of the World," the county has reinvented itself as a hub for fiber optics, data centers, and advanced manufacturing while preserving its deep industrial roots.

Companies like Corning and CommScope have made Catawba County the epicenter of America's fiber optic cable production, earning the region the nickname "Fiber Corridor." Catawba Valley Medical Center anchors the healthcare sector, and legacy furniture makers like Century Furniture and HSM Solutions continue to manufacture in the county. That blend of old-school manufacturing and new-economy tech creates a diverse business landscape that needs clean, reliable bookkeeping.

History & Economic Identity

Catawba County's economic identity was forged in furniture. For decades, the Hickory-Newton corridor was the undisputed "Furniture Capital of the World," with hundreds of manufacturers producing everything from upholstered sofas to hardwood dining sets. Companies like Broyhill, Century Furniture, Bernhardt, and Hickory Chair built global reputations from factories right here in the county.

When globalization disrupted the furniture industry, Catawba County pivoted. Corning Incorporated and CommScope anchored a new fiber optics and telecommunications manufacturing corridor, transforming the region into America's leading producer of fiber optic cable. Meanwhile, Apple and Google built massive data centers in the Maiden area, drawn by reliable power and fiber infrastructure.

Today, Catawba County balances its manufacturing legacy with a diversified economy. The SALT Block arts district in downtown Hickory signals a cultural renaissance. Catawba Valley Medical Center anchors healthcare. And a new generation of entrepreneurs is launching restaurants, breweries, tech startups, and professional services firms across the Hickory Metro.
