📍 About Michigan

The Heart of the American Auto Industry.

Michigan is the heart of the American automotive industry, home to General Motors, Ford, and Stellantis and a vast supplier network, but its economy reaches well beyond cars. Advanced manufacturing, agriculture (it's a leading producer of tart cherries and many specialty crops), furniture and office products (Grand Rapids), Great Lakes tourism, and a growing tech and mobility sector around Ann Arbor and Detroit all add depth.

The MI tax picture

Michigan levies a flat individual income tax of 4.25% (as of 2026, confirm the current rate). Its signature quirk is local: about two dozen Michigan cities impose their own city income tax on top of the state rate. Detroit's is the highest, roughly 2.4% for residents and 1.2% for nonresidents who work in the city, with others in Grand Rapids, Lansing, Flint, and more. The state sales tax is 6% with no local sales taxes. Pass-through profits flow to owners' returns, while C-corporations pay Michigan corporate income tax.
