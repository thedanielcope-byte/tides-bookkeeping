The AL Tax Angle

A high combined sales tax — and local occupational taxes.

Alabama pairs a low 4% state sales tax with county and city add-ons that make Tuscaloosa County's combined rates among the highest in the country, plus a graduated income tax topping at 5%. Some cities also levy an occupational tax on wages earned in the city. We keep your combined sales tax and payroll withholding clean.
