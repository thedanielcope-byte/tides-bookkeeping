The AR Tax Angle

A fast-falling income tax — and a high combined sales tax.

Arkansas pairs a fast-falling income tax (top rate now 3.7% for 2026, down from 6% in 2021) with a 6.5% state sales tax that stacks with county and city add-ons — Faulkner County businesses often charge combined rates in the high single or low double digits. The state dropped its grocery tax in 2026, but local grocery taxes still apply. We keep your combined sales tax and books clean.
