📍 About Yakima County

The Fruit and Hop Bowl of the Nation.

Yakima County is one of the most productive agricultural regions in the country — the source of the majority of U.S. hops, plus apples, cherries, and a fast-growing wine industry in the Yakima Valley AVA — supported by a deep food-processing, packing, and shipping economy.

We serve businesses across the county — Yakima, and the smaller communities in between.

The WA Tax Angle

No income tax — but the B&O tax is the catch.

Washington has no personal income tax, but businesses owe the state Business & Occupation (B&O) tax on gross receipts — total revenue, with no deduction for costs — at rates that vary by activity (as of 2026; confirm current rates). Add high combined sales tax and, in many cities, a local B&O tax on top, and accurate gross-revenue tracking matters more here than in most states. That is exactly what we keep clean for Yakima County businesses.
