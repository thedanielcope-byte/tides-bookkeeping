📍 About Missouri

The Show-Me State.

Missouri sits at the crossroads of America, with a diverse economy spanning aerospace and defense (Boeing's large presence in St. Louis), transportation and logistics, agriculture, financial services, healthcare, and beer and food manufacturing (Anheuser-Busch is headquartered in St. Louis). The metro areas of Kansas City and St. Louis anchor commerce, while Springfield and Columbia add healthcare, education, and services. Its central location and low cost of living make it a logistics and distribution hub.

The MO tax picture

Missouri has been steadily cutting its income tax. The top individual income tax rate is 4.7% for 2026, down from 5.3% a few years earlier, and the state continues to reduce it through revenue-triggered cuts, with proposals to eliminate it entirely under discussion (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). The state sales tax is a low 4.225%, but local add-ons push the average combined rate to roughly 8.4% (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). Because most small businesses are pass-throughs, that falling income-tax rate flows straight to the owner's return. We keep your books current so you always know where your taxable profit stands.
