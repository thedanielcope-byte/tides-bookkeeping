📍 About Utah

Home of the Silicon Slopes.

Utah has one of the fastest-growing and most diversified economies in the country, anchored by the 'Silicon Slopes' tech corridor that runs from Salt Lake City through Lehi and Provo and is home to companies like Adobe and Qualtrics along with a deep bench of startups. Beyond tech, the state leans on financial services, aerospace and defense, mining, higher education, and an outdoor-recreation and tourism sector built around five national parks. Its young, highly educated workforce and low unemployment have made Utah a magnet for business relocation.

The UT tax picture

Utah keeps its tax code simple with a single flat individual income tax rate, currently around 4.5% and trending lower after several years of cuts (as of 2026 - confirm current rate before filing). That same flat rate applies to corporate income, and there is no separate franchise tax or gross-receipts tax. Sales tax starts at a 4.85% state rate plus a mandatory statewide local add-on (a combined base of roughly 6.1%), with additional local options pushing the average to about 7.2% (as of 2026 - confirm current rate). Overall, Utah is regarded as one of the more business-friendly tax environments in the nation.
