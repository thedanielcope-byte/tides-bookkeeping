The MA Tax Angle

A flat income tax — plus the millionaire's surtax.

Massachusetts businesses in Worcester County pay a flat 5% state income tax — with a 4% surtax on income above roughly $1 million — and collect a flat 6.25% state sales tax that has no local add-on anywhere in the state. Corporations also owe the state's corporate excise. We keep your income, sales, and corporate-excise records clean.
