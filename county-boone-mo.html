📍 About Boone County

The University County.

Boone County sits midway between Kansas City and St. Louis &mdash; home to the University of Missouri, a major health-care and research base, and a large insurance and services sector, anchored by Columbia.

We serve businesses across the county — Columbia, and the smaller communities in between.

The MO Tax Angle

A low state sales tax — and the city earnings tax.

Missouri's state sales tax is a low 4.225%, but Boone County's local add-ons push combined rates toward 8-11%, and the state income tax keeps falling on annual revenue triggers (top rate now 4.7%, headed for 4.5%). We keep your combined sales tax and books clean.
