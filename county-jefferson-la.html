📍 About Jefferson Parish

The New Orleans Metro Parish.

Jefferson Parish is the populous suburban core of the New Orleans metro &mdash; home to Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport, a deep base of retail, logistics, and healthcare, and the cities of Kenner and Metairie.

We serve businesses across the county — Kenner, and the smaller communities in between.

The LA Tax Angle

The nation's highest sales taxes — and a new flat income tax.

Louisiana has the highest average combined sales-tax rate in the nation — the state rate rose to 5% in 2025 and, once parish and city taxes are added, Jefferson Parish businesses often pay a combined rate above 10% — and sales tax is collected parish by parish, one of the most complex systems in the country. On the income side, Louisiana just flattened its tax to a low 3%. We keep your parish sales tax and books clean.
