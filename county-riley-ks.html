The KS Tax Angle

A big grocery-tax cut — and a high combined sales tax.

Kansas fully eliminated its state sales tax on groceries in 2025 (local grocery taxes still apply), while keeping a 6.5% state rate on other goods that stacks with local add-ons — Riley County businesses often charge combined rates toward 8-10%. The income tax runs in two brackets (5.2% and a 5.58% top rate). We keep your combined sales tax and books clean.
