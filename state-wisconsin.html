📍 About Wisconsin

America's Dairyland.

Wisconsin is one of the nation's manufacturing and agricultural leaders - 'America's Dairyland' produces much of the country's cheese, while factories across the state build machinery, paper, food products, and consumer goods for names like Harley-Davidson, Kohler, and Oshkosh. Milwaukee and Madison anchor growing healthcare, insurance, biotech, and technology sectors. Family farms, food processing, and mid-sized manufacturers form the core of the small-business economy.

The WI tax picture

Wisconsin has a graduated individual income tax ranging from 3.5% up to a top rate of 7.65% on higher incomes (as of 2026 - confirm current rate before filing). Its sales tax is among the lowest in the nation: a 5% state rate plus an optional 0.5% county tax in most counties (about 5.5% combined), with a few areas adding small stadium or resort taxes. Wisconsin also offers a generous Manufacturing and Agriculture Credit that can nearly eliminate state income tax on qualifying manufacturing and farm income. One quirk worth knowing: businesses register with the Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions rather than a Secretary of State.
