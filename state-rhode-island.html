📍 About Rhode Island

The Ocean State's Flat 7%.

Despite being the smallest state, Rhode Island has a diverse economy. Healthcare is the dominant employer, anchored by major hospital networks, and higher education is a close second with Brown University, the University of Rhode Island, RISD, and Providence College. Marine and defense activity centers on Naval Station Newport — home to the Naval War College — and General Dynamics Electric Boat, while tourism along the coast and Newport's mansions, plus legacy jewelry and textile manufacturing, round out the Ocean State.

The RI tax picture

Rhode Island keeps its sales tax refreshingly simple: a flat 7% statewide, one of the highest flat rates in the country, with no local city or county add-ons — what you see is what you collect (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). The personal income tax is graduated across three brackets, running from 3.75% up to a top rate of 5.99% (as of 2026 — confirm current rates). Businesses organized as corporations pay a flat 7% corporate income tax, subject to a $400 minimum tax even in an unprofitable year. Because there's no local sales tax layer to untangle, Rhode Island's rules are more straightforward than most — the main things to watch are the income tax brackets and that minimum corporate tax.
