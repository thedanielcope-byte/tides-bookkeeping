The WV Tax Angle

A falling income tax — and the local B&O tax.

Beyond West Virginia's falling income tax (top rate 4.82% for 2026) and its 6% sales tax, the tax that catches Cabell County businesses off guard is local: many West Virginia cities levy a Business & Occupation (B&O) tax on gross receipts, with no deduction for the cost of goods or labor. We keep your books ready for the B&O and your state filings clean.
