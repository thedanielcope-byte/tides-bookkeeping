📍 About Connecticut

The Insurance Capital of the World.

Connecticut has one of the highest per-capita incomes in the nation, anchored by finance and insurance. Hartford is known as the Insurance Capital of the World, home to major insurers, while Fairfield County (Stamford and Greenwich) hosts hedge funds and corporate headquarters within commuting distance of New York City. The state is also a defense-manufacturing heavyweight — Pratt & Whitney jet engines, Electric Boat submarines, and Sikorsky helicopters — with strong pharmaceutical, bioscience, and higher-education sectors led by Yale.

The CT tax picture

Connecticut is a high-tax Northeastern state. Its individual income tax is graduated from 2% up to a top rate of 6.99% (as of 2026 — confirm the current rate before filing). Sales tax is a flat 6.35% statewide with no local sales taxes — a rare simplicity — though higher rates apply to certain items (such as a 7.75% rate on some luxury goods and a 1% surcharge on prepared meals). Connecticut also carries some of the highest property taxes in the country and an estate tax. For business owners, the uniform statewide sales tax is a relief, but the overall tax burden is high.
