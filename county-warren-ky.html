The KY Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and local occupational taxes.

Beyond Kentucky's flat state income tax (3.5% in 2026) and its 6% sales tax with no local add-on, the tax that catches Warren County businesses off guard is local: cities and counties across Kentucky levy occupational license taxes on payroll and business net profits, and a business can owe both a city and the county on the same income — with a credit against the county tax where state law provides one. We track which local occupational taxes apply to you and keep each return clean.
