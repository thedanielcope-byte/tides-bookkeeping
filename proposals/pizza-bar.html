Where you're starting

A strong restaurant, with books that quietly fell behind.

Pizza Bar is a healthy operation — roughly $1.5 million a year in sales, with solid fundamentals. The challenge isn't the restaurant; it's the bookkeeping. The books were kept current through February, then stopped being maintained in March.

Today that means reconciliations are about five to six months behind, roughly $407,000 is sitting in undeposited funds, and payroll and rent aren't being recorded — which makes the current financials overstate profit by around $220,000. None of that reflects how the business is actually doing. It's all fixable.

Rebuild it clean, then keep it that way.
