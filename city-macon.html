📍 About Macon, GA

The City Behind the Business.

Macon is more than a zip code — it's one of Georgia's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1823 on the fall line of the Ocmulgee River, Macon was built as a trading center serving Georgia's cotton-producing interior. The city's location made it a natural crossroads — connected by river, rail, and eventually highway to every corner of the state. During the Civil War, Macon served as a Confederate arsenal and was one of the few Georgia cities to avoid destruction.

The 20th century brought institutional growth and cultural fame: Robins Air Force Base was established in 1942 and grew into the state's largest single-site employer, Mercer University expanded from a regional college into a comprehensive research university, and Macon became the "Song of the South" — birthplace of Little Richard, Otis Redding, and the Allman Brothers Band. GEICO's major operations center brought thousands of corporate jobs.

Today that central position continues to attract business — with more small business owners than ever, and more who need clean books to grow with confidence.
