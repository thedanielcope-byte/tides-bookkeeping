About the Area

Florence County Is
SC's Pee Dee
Regional Hub.

Florence County sits at the crossroads of I-95 and I-20, making it the commercial and healthcare hub for South Carolina's Pee Dee region. The City of Florence serves as the retail, medical, and services center for a multi-county area.

McLeod Health is the county's largest employer and a regional healthcare system. Francis Marion University provides educational depth. The I-95/I-20 interchange makes Florence a major logistics and distribution hub, attracting warehousing, trucking, and supply chain businesses.

History & Economic Identity

Florence County's strategic location at the intersection of I-95 and I-20 has shaped its economic identity as the Pee Dee region's commercial center. Originally a railroad junction, Florence evolved into a retail and healthcare hub serving northeastern South Carolina.

McLeod Health has grown from a single hospital into a regional healthcare system employing thousands. Francis Marion University provides a steady pipeline of graduates. The I-95 corridor attracts logistics, distribution, and hospitality businesses.

Today, Florence County serves as the go-to destination for retail, healthcare, and professional services for a multi-county area. The combination of highway accessibility, healthcare employment, and agricultural roots creates a diverse business ecosystem.
