The Financial Headaches
We Fix

Healthcare practices face financial challenges that general bookkeepers routinely miss. Here's what we hear from nearly every new client.

😩

Insurance Reimbursements Are Unpredictable

Payments come from a dozen different payers on different schedules. Reconciling what you billed vs. what you received vs. what's still outstanding is a full-time job on its own.

📉

Compliance Risk Keeps You Up at Night

Healthcare financials face more scrutiny than almost any other industry. Sloppy books aren't just inefficient — they're a liability. We keep your records audit-ready at all times.

🧾

Payroll Is More Complex Than Most

Provider compensation models, hygienist splits, front office staff — healthcare payroll involves multiple structures and pay types that general bookkeepers often mishandle.

😰

No Clear Picture of Practice Profitability

Between collections, write-offs, and overhead, it's hard to know what your practice is actually netting. Without clean monthly financials, you're making decisions without data.
