📍 About Newport News, VA

Shipbuilding on the Peninsula.

Newport News is anchored by Newport News Shipbuilding &mdash; the nation's largest industrial employer of its kind, and the only builder of U.S. Navy aircraft carriers &mdash; along with a deep base of defense and aerospace suppliers, the Port, Jefferson Lab, and nearby NASA Langley. Its small-business economy serves that industrial base plus healthcare, retail, and professional services.

Virginia's real tax complexity is local. Nearly every Virginia locality levies the BPOL tax (Business, Professional & Occupational License) — a tax on your gross receipts, not your profit, with rates that vary by business classification and by locality and are self-reported each March. Newport News businesses also deal with the Business Tangible Personal Property tax on equipment. On top of that, Virginia's income tax tops out at 5.75% (on income over just $17,000) and sales tax runs from 5.3% to 7% depending on the region. Keeping your gross receipts tracked by BPOL classification is exactly what we do.
