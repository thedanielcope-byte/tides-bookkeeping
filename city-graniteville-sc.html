📍 About Graniteville, SC

The Village Behind the Business.

Graniteville is a small, history-rich community in Aiken County and the CSRA. Understanding its mill-village roots, the Aiken-Augusta corridor around it, and the businesses that actually operate here is how we serve our local clients better.

History & Economy

Graniteville was founded in 1845 by William Gregg as the South's first large-scale, successful cotton mill village — a planned community of mill, housing, school, and church that became a model for Southern industrialization. The Gregg-Granite Historic District is on the National Register, and many of the original village structures still stand today.

Graniteville is also remembered for the January 2005 Norfolk Southern train derailment and chlorine spill, which killed nine people and forced a major evacuation — a tragedy that shaped the modern community's resilience and emergency-response standards.

Today the local economy runs on small businesses, contractors, retail along US-1, light manufacturing (some of it still rooted in the textile heritage), agriculture, and a strong base of Savannah River Site (SRS) commuters who live in and around the village. Sitting just west of Aiken — the county seat — and minutes from North Augusta and the Augusta GA metro, Graniteville's economic gravity pulls in every direction across the CSRA.

Graniteville Neighborhoods & Areas

Gregg-Granite Historic Mill Village
The 1845 William Gregg mill village — National Register district of historic mill housing, the original mill complex, and the village core.
Hickman / Hampton Avenue
Older residential streets near Hickman House (William Gregg's home) — quiet, walkable, with small service businesses operating from home and along the avenue.
Vaucluse-Adjacent
Just west of the village toward Vaucluse — mill-village heritage continues here with light industrial and contractor-based businesses.
Brookhaven
Established residential pocket on the Graniteville side — a mix of homes and small home-based businesses serving the surrounding area.
US-1 Corridor
The main commercial spine through Graniteville — auto, retail, food, contractors, and service businesses lining the Aiken-to-North-Augusta route.
Bettis Academy Road / SC-421
Heritage corridor named for the historic Bettis Academy & Junior College — rural-edge residential with small contractors, farms, and service operators.

Surrounding CSRA Communities

Aiken
The Aiken County seat just east of Graniteville — historic horse country, downtown retail, and the dominant local commercial hub.
North Augusta
Riverfront city minutes west on US-1 — strong residential and small-business growth tied to the Augusta GA metro.
Vaucluse & Warrenville
Small mill-heritage communities just west of Graniteville along US-1 — tight-knit, with contractors and trades-based small businesses.
Beech Island
Rural southern Aiken County community near the Savannah River — agriculture, small manufacturing, and home of Redcliffe Plantation State Park.
Jackson
Town between Graniteville and the Savannah River — SRS commuter community with a small but steady local business base.
Augusta, GA
Across the Savannah River — the CSRA's major metro and a regular market for Graniteville-based contractors, vendors, and service businesses.
