The OK Tax Angle

A modest income tax — and high, city-by-city sales taxes.

Because Oklahoma cities fund their operations through local sales tax rather than property tax, combined sales-tax rates across Garfield County run high and vary from city to city — the 4.5% state rate plus city and county add-ons. Add a top income-tax rate cut to 4.5% for 2026 and the 2024 removal of the state grocery tax (local still applies), and clean sales-tax tracking by jurisdiction is essential. That's exactly what we keep straight.
