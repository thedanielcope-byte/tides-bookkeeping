About the Area

Mahoning County:
The Valley,
Rebuilding.

Mahoning County sits at the center of the Mahoning Valley, a historic steel-making region anchored by Youngstown. Once one of America's great steel capitals, the county has spent the last generation rebuilding its economy around advanced manufacturing, healthcare, education, and a growing auto and EV corridor.

Youngstown State University, Mercy Health, and the Lordstown-area auto and EV plants just up the road are driving the Valley's next chapter. For local businesses, Youngstown's income tax and the surrounding RITA communities make clean, location-aware bookkeeping a meaningful edge.

History & Economic Identity

Mahoning County was one of the steel-making capitals of the world. Youngstown Sheet and Tube and a chain of mills along the Mahoning River made the Valley an industrial titan for most of the 20th century — until the dramatic mill closures of the late 1970s reshaped the region almost overnight.

The decades since have been a story of reinvention. Youngstown State University grew into an economic anchor, downtown Youngstown became a nationally noted small-business and tech incubator hub, and the Valley's manufacturing base diversified beyond raw steel.

The next chapter is being written along the Lordstown auto and EV corridor just north in Trumbull County, drawing suppliers and workers back to the region. Across Mahoning County, small businesses — from Boardman retail to Austintown trades — work within Youngstown's income tax and the surrounding RITA communities.
