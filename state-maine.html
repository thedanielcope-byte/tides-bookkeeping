📍 About Maine

Lobster, Forests, and Vacationland.

Maine's economy is shaped by its coast and its forests. It lands the vast majority of the nation's lobster, supports a large tourism and hospitality sector (its license plates read Vacationland), and remains a leader in forestry, paper, and wild blueberry and potato production. Shipbuilding (Bath Iron Works), healthcare, and a growing food-and-beverage scene add further depth.

The ME tax picture

Maine has a graduated individual income tax with three brackets, roughly 5.8%, 6.75%, and a top rate of 7.15% (as of 2026, confirm the current rates and bracket thresholds, which adjust for inflation). The state sales tax is 5.5%, and Maine allows no local or county sales taxes, so the rate is uniform statewide, though certain items such as prepared meals, lodging, and short-term rentals carry higher special rates. Pass-through business profits flow to owners' individual returns, while C-corporations pay Maine corporate income tax.
