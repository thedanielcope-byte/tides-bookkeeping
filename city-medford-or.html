📍 About Medford, OR

Healthcare, Pears, and the Rogue Valley.

Medford is the hub of Southern Oregon's Rogue Valley — a regional health-care center (Asante and Providence), the home of Harry & David and a pear-growing and food-processing heritage, and the retail and distribution hub for the region. Its downtown anchors a small-business economy of healthcare, agriculture services, and retail.

Oregon is one of only five states with no sales tax at all — instead it leans on income. There's a graduated income tax that tops out at 9.9%, one of the highest rates in the country, plus a statewide Corporate Activity Tax (0.57% on a business's Oregon commercial activity above $1 million, with a deduction for labor or cost of goods). For Medford businesses, there's no sales tax to collect — so the work is keeping income and the Corporate Activity Tax clean, and that's exactly what we do.
