The Financial Headaches
We Fix

Solo business owners face unique financial challenges. Here's what we hear from nearly every new client.

😩

Business and Personal Finances Are Mixed

Running business expenses through a personal account is the number one bookkeeping mistake solo business owners make. It costs you at tax time and makes your books useless for decisions.

📉

Quarterly Taxes Catch You Off Guard

Self-employment tax is 15.3% on top of income tax — and it's due four times a year. Without tracking your income monthly, the quarterly bill always feels like a surprise.

🧾

You're Missing Deductions

Home office, phone, internet, subscriptions, equipment, mileage, professional development — solopreneurs have dozens of deductions that go unclaimed without organized records.

😰

Tax Time Is a Scramble Every Year

Pulling together a year of receipts, transactions, and invoices in April is stressful and expensive. Monthly bookkeeping makes tax season a non-event.
