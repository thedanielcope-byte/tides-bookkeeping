About the Area

Cobb County Is
Metro Atlanta's
Western Powerhouse.

Cobb County is the third most populous county in Georgia and one of the most economically diverse areas in metro Atlanta. Anchored by Marietta -- the county seat -- Cobb blends a thriving suburban economy with major corporate headquarters, defense installations, and a fast-growing entertainment sector.

Home to Lockheed Martin's massive F-35 production facility at Dobbins Air Reserve Base, The Home Depot's corporate headquarters in Vinings, and Truist Park (home of the Atlanta Braves), Cobb County is a powerhouse of employment and small business activity.

History & Economic Identity

Cobb County's modern identity was shaped by two forces: the defense industry and suburban growth. The establishment of the Bell Aircraft Corporation (now Lockheed Martin) during World War II transformed the county from a quiet agricultural region into a defense manufacturing powerhouse.

Lockheed Martin remains one of the county's largest employers, producing F-35 fighter jets at its massive Marietta facility adjacent to Dobbins Air Reserve Base. The Home Depot moved its corporate headquarters to the Vinings area of Cobb County, bringing thousands of corporate jobs and a ripple effect of professional service businesses.

Today, Cobb County is one of the most dynamic suburban economies in the Southeast. The Battery Atlanta mixed-use district around Truist Park has created a new entertainment and business hub, Kennesaw State University has become Georgia's third-largest school, and the I-75 corridor from Smyrna through Marietta to Kennesaw is one of metro Atlanta's most active commercial spines.

Key Corridors & Communities

Marietta Square / Downtown
The historic heart of Cobb County -- restaurants, law firms, boutiques, and a vibrant weekend market scene around the historic square.
I-75 / Cobb Parkway Corridor
The commercial spine of the county running from Smyrna through Marietta to Kennesaw. Dense with auto dealers, retail, restaurants, and service businesses.
Vinings / Cumberland
The corporate hub of Cobb County -- Home Depot HQ, Truist Park, and the Battery Atlanta mixed-use district with offices, restaurants, and hotels.
Kennesaw / Town Center
Anchored by KSU and Town Center at Cobb mall -- tech firms, student-focused businesses, and growing professional services along Barrett Parkway.
Smyrna / Market Village
A revitalized downtown with restaurants, breweries, and creative businesses. One of metro Atlanta's most walkable suburban districts.
Acworth / Lake Allatoona
Northwestern Cobb County's lake community -- outdoor recreation, waterfront dining, and a charming Main Street retail district.
Powder Springs / Austell
Western Cobb's commercial areas with trades, home services, and growing retail along Veterans Memorial Hwy.
East Cobb / Roswell Rd
Affluent residential areas with professional services, medical practices, and upscale retail serving the East Cobb community.
