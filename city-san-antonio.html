📍 About San Antonio, TX

The City Behind the Business.

San Antonio is more than a zip code — it's one of Texas's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1718 as a Spanish mission and civil settlement, San Antonio is one of the oldest cities in Texas. The Battle of the Alamo in 1836 — where a small band of Texan defenders held the mission against the Mexican army — became a defining moment in Texas history and American lore.

The late 19th century brought railroads and cattle, establishing San Antonio as a regional trade center. Military investment began with Fort Sam Houston (1876) and expanded dramatically during both World Wars, making San Antonio the "Military City, USA." The River Walk — developed after a devastating 1921 flood — evolved into one of America's most visited attractions. USAA headquartered here in 1922 and grew into a Fortune 500 financial giant. The Toyota manufacturing plant (2006), the San Antonio cybersecurity corridor, and the growth of Methodist Healthcare and Baptist Health System diversified the economy well beyond military and tourism.

Today that blend of military heritage and modern growth continues — with more small business owners than ever, and more who need clean books to grow with confidence.
