The NM Tax Angle

A graduated income tax — and the Gross Receipts Tax.

In Eddy County, New Mexico's Gross Receipts Tax (GRT) — not a sales tax — is the thing to get right: it taxes the seller's gross receipts, including most services, at a destination-based combined rate (the 4.875% state rate plus local add-ons). Add a graduated income tax topping at 5.9%, and clean GRT tracking by location is essential. That's exactly what we keep straight.
