Text message opt-in

Tides Bookkeeping LLC texts people only after they opt in on our website. There are two kinds of texts, and each has its own separate, optional, unchecked checkbox. Neither is required to contact us or to buy anything.

Box 1 · Service texts

Appointment reminders, replies to your inquiry, and account and document requests.

By checking this box, you agree to receive text messages from Tides Bookkeeping at the number provided, including appointment reminders, replies to your inquiry, and account and document requests. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
Box 2 · Marketing texts (separate)

Occasional offers and new-service announcements, up to 4 per month. Sent only if this second box is checked.

Optional, separate from the box above: check this box to also receive occasional marketing texts from Tides Bookkeeping, such as offers and new-service announcements. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Up to 4 msgs/month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
