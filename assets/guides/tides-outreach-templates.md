# PHASE 4: DAILY SEND VOLUME & PACING

## Recommended Daily Limits

| Channel | Daily Volume | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Pre-call emails | 30-50/day | Stagger send times (9am, 11am batches) |
| Cold calls | 40-60/day | Using JustCall power dialer |
| SMS (post-call only) | Matches call volume | Only sent after a call attempt |
| Follow-up emails | Auto via GHL workflows | No manual work needed |

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | Load new contacts → Pre-call Email 1 goes out |
| Tuesday | Batch 2 of pre-call emails if needed |
| Wednesday | Email 2 (Day 3) fires for Monday's batch → Cold calls start for Monday batch |
| Thursday | Cold calls continue + new batch Email 1 |
| Friday | Follow-up calls (maybes, no-answers) + new batch starts |

## Ramp-Up Schedule

| Week | New Contacts/Week | Active in Sequences |
|------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Week 1-2 | 50 | 50 |
| Week 3-4 | 100 | ~150 |
| Month 2 | 150 | ~300 |
| Month 3+ | 200 | ~400-500 |

Start small, prove the sequences work, then scale. Don't flood 500 contacts on day 1 — your sender reputation matters and you need to see what converts before scaling.
