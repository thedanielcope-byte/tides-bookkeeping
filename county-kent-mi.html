📍 About Kent County

West Michigan's Hub.

Kent County anchors West Michigan around Grand Rapids — furniture manufacturing (Steelcase, MillerKnoll, Haworth), a major healthcare and life-sciences sector along the Medical Mile, craft beer, and a dense, entrepreneurial small-business economy.

We serve businesses across the county — Grand Rapids, and the smaller communities in between.

The MI Tax Angle

A flat state income tax — and local city income taxes.

Kent County businesses handle Michigan's flat 4.25% state income tax and statewide 6% sales tax — and employers in Grand Rapids must also withhold the city's income tax (1.5% for residents, 0.75% for non-residents). We keep your state and Grand Rapids city withholding right for every employee.
