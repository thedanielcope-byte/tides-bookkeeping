Where you’re starting

The stores are working. The books need to catch up.

Wolverine Commerce runs three Shopify stores under one Texas LLC: Ace Garage Storage since January 2023, Duramax Sheds Online since October 2024, and Duramax Furniture, which just launched. Ace and Sheds have done about $1.5 million in net sales so far this year, roughly half each, at around $1,000 an order and 70–90 orders a month per store.

2025 is done and filed. You already have QuickBooks Online Plus, with the Shopify connector running on Ace and Sheds. What’s missing is someone keeping it right every month. The plan below cleans up January through September 2026, splits the books by store, and gives you monthly numbers you can act on starting October 1.

What we’ll fix

Four things to fix, and to build in going forward.

01
One set of books, but no view by store
All three stores are DBAs of one LLC, so one QuickBooks file is the right call. QuickBooks Plus can tag every sale and cost by store (it calls these classes), so you get a separate P&L for Ace, Sheds and Furniture plus the combined total. Costs that serve the whole company, like software, stay company-wide.
02
Every Shopify payout matched to the bank
The QuickBooks Shopify connector posts every order on its own. Without care, that can double-count sales against the bank deposit or bury Shopify’s fees. The first thing the cleanup checks is how it’s been landing in your file. From there, every payout ties to the bank to the penny, with sales, fees, refunds and shipping split out.
03
Returns, partial payments and chargebacks
On $1,000 orders with freight, a return or a partial refund moves real money. Each one gets recorded against the right store and the right month, so your sales numbers are net of what actually came back.
04
Sales tax outside Texas and Florida
The Sheds store collects sales tax in Texas and Florida. Shipping nationwide at your volume, you have likely passed the sales tax registration threshold in other states. I don’t file sales tax, but clean sales by state are the first thing your CPA or a sales tax specialist will need to sort it out. You’ll have them.

Your contractors are tracked in the portal all year, and in January I can prepare and file their 1099s for you. I don’t replace your CPA; I’m the monthly engine that feeds them.
