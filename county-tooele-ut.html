📍 About Tooele County

The Gateway to the West Desert.

Tooele County stretches west of the Salt Lake Valley into the West Desert &mdash; a fast-growing county anchored by distribution and logistics at the Utah Industrial Depot, a mining and defense heritage, and residential growth spilling out from the metro.

We serve businesses across the county — Tooele, and the smaller communities in between.

The UT Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and layered local sales taxes.

Utah pairs a flat 4.45% income tax (2026, individuals and corporations alike) with a layered sales tax: the 4.85% state rate stacks with county, city, and mass-transit options that vary by location across Tooele County, and Utah still taxes groceries at a reduced rate. We keep your sales tax tracked by jurisdiction and your books clean.
