📍 About Cleveland, TN

The City Behind the Business.

Cleveland is more than a zip code — it's one of Tennessee's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1836 and named for Colonel Benjamin Cleveland, a Revolutionary War hero, the city grew as the county seat of Bradley County. Its location in the fertile Ocoee River valley made it an agricultural center, and the arrival of the railroad in the 1850s brought commercial growth.

Cleveland became the birthplace and world headquarters of the Church of God denomination in 1886, establishing the city's distinctive faith-based identity. Lee University, founded in 1918 as a Church of God school, has grown into a respected liberal arts university. Manufacturing arrived in the mid-20th century, with Whirlpool (formerly Magic Chef) becoming the city's largest private employer.

That unique mix of faith, education, and manufacturing means a diverse base of small business owners — and small business owners who need clean books to grow with confidence.
