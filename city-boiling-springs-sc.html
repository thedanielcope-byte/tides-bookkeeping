📍 About Boiling Springs, SC

The Community Behind the Business.

Boiling Springs isn't a city — it's an unincorporated CDP north of Spartanburg, anchored by a strong school district, a busy SC-9 corridor, and miles of new residential growth. Knowing the area is how we serve our clients better.

History & Community Growth

Boiling Springs is named for the natural boiling, effervescent springs that bubbled up in the area long before the modern community took shape. Settled as a rural farming community north of Spartanburg, it grew steadily through the 20th century around Mt. Olive Baptist Church and the local school district.

Today, Boiling Springs is one of the fastest-growing CDPs in the Upstate — a bedroom community for Spartanburg, the BMW corridor in Greer, and the Inland Port. It remains unincorporated, with its identity tied to Spartanburg School District 2 and Boiling Springs High School (the Bulldogs) — one of the largest high schools in South Carolina, with about 3,500 students.

That growth has filled the SC-9 corridor with new contractors, retail, restaurants, and small professional offices — and brought a steady stream of new small business owners who need their books done right.
