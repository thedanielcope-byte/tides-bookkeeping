The MD Tax Angle

A local piggyback income tax — and a flat 6% sales tax.

Businesses in Carroll County pay a graduated Maryland state income tax (up to 5.75%, with new 6.25% and 6.5% brackets on high incomes as of 2025) plus the county's own 'piggyback' local income tax — Maryland counties levy 2.25% to 3.30% of taxable income. The sales tax is a flat 6% statewide with no local add-on, plus a new 3% tax on certain data and IT services. We keep your local income tax and sales tax clean.
