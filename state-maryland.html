📍 About Maryland

Biotech, Cybersecurity, and the Chesapeake.

Maryland's economy leans heavily on knowledge and government. Its proximity to Washington, D.C. anchors a large federal, defense, and cybersecurity presence (Fort Meade and the NSA, plus a dense contractor base), while institutions like Johns Hopkins and the NIH make it a national leader in biosciences and healthcare. The Port of Baltimore, aerospace, and professional services round out one of the wealthiest state economies per capita.

The MD tax picture

Maryland has a graduated individual income tax, from 2% up to a longstanding top state rate around 5.75%, with newer high-income brackets reaching roughly 6.25% to 6.5% (confirm the current rates and thresholds for 2026). Its signature feature is the local 'piggyback' income tax: every county and Baltimore City levies its own income tax, roughly 1.75% to 3.2%, filed and paid on the same Maryland return, so combined state-plus-local income tax can approach 9%. The state sales tax is 6% with no local sales taxes.
