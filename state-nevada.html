📍 About Nevada

No State Income Tax in the Silver State.

Nevada's economy is powered by its world-famous tourism, gaming, and hospitality industry centered on the Las Vegas Strip and Reno, along with fast-growing logistics and distribution, mining (gold and lithium), and a rising tech and manufacturing presence, including Tesla's Gigafactory at the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center. Las Vegas and Henderson dominate the population, while Reno and Sparks anchor the north. Small businesses thrive on the state's pro-business, low-tax reputation.

The NV tax picture

Nevada has no personal income tax — its constitution prohibits one — which is a major draw for entrepreneurs and relocating business owners. Instead, the state raises revenue through a 6.85% state sales tax (average combined rate around 8.2%), gaming taxes, and the Commerce Tax, a gross-receipts tax that applies only to businesses with more than $4 million in Nevada gross revenue in a fiscal year, at low industry-specific rates (as of 2026 — confirm current rates and thresholds). Most small businesses fall under the Commerce Tax threshold but still file a Modified Business (payroll) Tax if they have employees. No income tax doesn't mean no bookkeeping — clean records are what prove your numbers.
