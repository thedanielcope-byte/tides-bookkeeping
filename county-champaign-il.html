The IL Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and high, variable local taxes.

Illinois pairs a flat 4.95% state income tax with a sales-tax system that stacks the 6.25% state rate with county, municipal, and special-district add-ons — so combined rates across Champaign County vary widely and rank among the nation's highest — plus the Personal Property Replacement Tax (PPRT) that every Illinois business entity owes on net income (2.5% corporations, 1.5% pass-throughs). We keep both straight.
