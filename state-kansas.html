📍 About Kansas

America's Wheat and Aviation Heartland.

Kansas pairs one of the nation's largest agricultural economies, as a top producer of wheat, grain sorghum, and beef cattle, with a globally significant aviation-manufacturing cluster centered on Wichita, long known as the Air Capital of the World and home to Textron Aviation, Bombardier Learjet, and Spirit AeroSystems. Energy (oil, gas, and wind) and transportation and logistics add depth, while the Kansas City metro around Overland Park anchors a growing professional-services and technology base.

The KS tax picture

As of 2026, Kansas taxes individual income under a two-bracket structure, roughly 5.2% on lower income and 5.58% above a set threshold, though you should confirm the current rates and thresholds before filing. The state sales tax is 6.5%, but city and county taxes stack on top, pushing average combined rates to roughly 8.8%, among the higher combined rates in the country. Kansas has eliminated its state sales tax on groceries. Pass-through business profits flow to owners' individual returns, while C-corporations pay a separate Kansas corporate income tax.
