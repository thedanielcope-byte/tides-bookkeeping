About the Area

Montgomery County:
Birthplace of
Aviation.

Montgomery County is the heart of the Dayton region and the birthplace of aviation, where the Wright brothers built and flew the first airplane. Today it blends a powerhouse aerospace-and-defense economy with advanced manufacturing, healthcare, and a resurgent downtown.

Wright-Patterson Air Force Base — the region's single largest employer — anchors a dense cluster of aerospace, defense, and research contractors, while Premier Health and the University of Dayton round out the base. For local firms, Dayton's self-administered income tax and the surrounding suburban levies make clean, location-aware books essential.

History & Economic Identity

Montgomery County is where flight was born. Wilbur and Orville Wright ran their bicycle shop in Dayton and did the engineering that led to the first powered flight in 1903 — and the region has been synonymous with aviation ever since. That legacy became institutional with the growth of what is now Wright-Patterson Air Force Base.

Dayton was also a great American invention town — home to the cash register (NCR), the electric self-starter, and countless patents per capita. Advanced manufacturing and research have long been the region's economic DNA, feeding a broad supplier and contractor base.

Today Wright-Patterson AFB anchors an aerospace-and-defense economy that ripples through thousands of local contractors, while Premier Health and the University of Dayton drive healthcare and research. Small businesses across Kettering, Centerville, and Huber Heights work within Dayton's income tax and the county's suburban municipal levies.
