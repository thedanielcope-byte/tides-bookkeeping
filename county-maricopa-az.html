📍 About Maricopa County

The Valley of the Sun.

Maricopa County is the beating heart of Arizona's economy and the fourth-most-populous county in the nation — the Valley of the Sun. Semiconductors (TSMC, Intel), aerospace and defense, finance and insurance, healthcare, and a booming logistics and tourism base anchor one of the fastest-growing metros in the country.

We serve businesses across the county — Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Scottsdale, Glendale, Tempe, and the smaller communities in between.

The AZ Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and the Transaction Privilege Tax.

Arizona's signature tax is the Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT) — not a true sales tax, but a tax on the seller for the privilege of doing business, levied on gross receipts. A Maricopa County business pays a combined rate built from the 5.6% state rate plus county and city rates, and the tax depends on which of Arizona's 16 business classifications applies. The Arizona Department of Revenue is the single point of filing, but getting the classification and rate right is where a lot of books go wrong — and it's exactly what we keep clean. Arizona also has a flat 2.5% state income tax, the lowest flat rate in the country.
