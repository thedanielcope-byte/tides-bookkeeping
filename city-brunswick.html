📍 About Brunswick, GA

The City Behind the Business.

Brunswick is more than a zip code — it's one of Georgia's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1771 by the colonial legislature, Brunswick was named after the House of Brunswick — the royal family of Britain's King George II. The city was planned on a grid system similar to Savannah's and grew as a port and lumber center in the 19th century, exporting naval stores, timber, and cotton from Georgia's coastal plantations.

The 20th century brought military and federal investment: the Glynco Naval Air Station operated during World War II, and after its closure in 1974, the site was converted into the Federal Law Enforcement Training Centers (FLETC) — now one of the largest federal training facilities in the country. The Port of Brunswick grew into America's top RoRo port, while the Golden Isles developed into one of the Southeast's premier resort destinations.

Today that blend of maritime, federal, and tourism economy continues — with small business owners who need clean books to grow with confidence.
