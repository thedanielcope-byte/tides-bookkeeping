The Financial Headaches
We Fix

Restaurant and bar finances have challenges that general bookkeepers routinely miss. Here's what we hear from nearly every new client.

😩

Margins Are Tight and Hard to See

You're busy every weekend, but are you actually profitable? Without tracking food cost percentages and labor ratios, you're running blind.

📉

Tip Reporting Is Confusing and High-Risk

Tip income, tip pools, tip-out to support staff — the IRS has specific rules and the penalties for mishandling are real. We track it correctly from day one.

Vendors and Bills Are Everywhere

Sysco, US Foods, local suppliers, alcohol distributors — vendor invoices pile up fast. We track every bill and payment so nothing gets missed or paid twice.

POS Data Doesn't Match the Bank

Your sales are in Toast, your money is in three bank accounts, and your cards settle on a two-day delay. Reconciling it all manually is a nightmare. We handle it.
