📍 About Minnesota

A Fortune 500 Powerhouse in the Land of 10,000 Lakes.

Minnesota anchors the Upper Midwest economy and is home to an unusually large cluster of Fortune 500 headquarters for its size, including UnitedHealth Group, Target, Best Buy, 3M, U.S. Bancorp, and General Mills. Beyond corporate headquarters, the state runs on healthcare and medical devices, with the Mayo Clinic and the Medical Alley corridor, plus agriculture and food processing, finance, and manufacturing. The Twin Cities of Minneapolis and Saint Paul form the commercial hub, while Rochester and Duluth add healthcare and shipping.

The MN tax picture

Minnesota is a high-tax state. It levies a graduated personal income tax with a top marginal rate of 9.85% (as of 2026 — confirm current rate), one of the highest in the country and reached at higher income levels. The statewide sales tax is 6.875%, and local option taxes can push the combined rate close to 9% in the Twin Cities metro (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). Minnesota also imposes a corporate franchise tax and an estate tax, so business owners and higher earners should plan carefully. Clean, current books make navigating Minnesota's brackets and filing requirements far less painful.
