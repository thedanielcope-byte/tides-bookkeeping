📍 About Massachusetts

The World's Life-Sciences Capital.

Massachusetts runs on brainpower. Anchored by Harvard, MIT, and dozens of other universities, the state is a global center of the life-sciences and biotech industry (Cambridge's Kendall Square) and a powerhouse in healthcare, higher education, technology, robotics, and financial services (Fidelity and State Street). It is one of the most innovation-dense and highest-income economies in the country.

The MA tax picture

Massachusetts levies a flat individual income tax of 5% on most income. Its signature feature is the voter-approved 'millionaire's tax,' a 4% surtax on annual income above roughly $1.1 million (the threshold indexes for inflation, about $1,108,000 for 2026, confirm the current figure), making the top marginal rate 9%. The state sales tax is 6.25% with no local sales taxes. Short-term capital gains are taxed at a higher rate than the general 5% (confirm current rates). Pass-through profits flow to owners' returns, while C-corporations pay the Massachusetts corporate excise.
