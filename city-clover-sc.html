📍 About Clover, SC

The Town Behind the Business.

Clover is a small York County town with a long history, a beloved school district, and a Main Street that's still the center of community life. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Local Economy

Clover got its name from the abundance of clover the town's founders found growing across the area when the community took shape in the late 1800s along what would become the rail line connecting York County to the Carolinas. Through much of the 20th century the town's economy revolved around small textile mills, farming, and the rail trade — and even as those industries faded, Clover held on to its small-town identity.

Today Clover is best known regionally for the Clover School District and the Clover Blue Eagles football tradition, both of which anchor the town's culture and draw families into the area. Lake Wylie just to the east, the Carolina border just to the north, and the Charlotte metro about 30 miles away have brought steady residential growth and a wave of new small businesses serving commuters, lake residents, and contractors working both sides of the state line.

That mix — local Main Street trade, lake-area services, and Charlotte-metro commuters — makes for an interesting set of bookkeeping needs. Tides is built to handle all of them.
