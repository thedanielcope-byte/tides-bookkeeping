📍 About Park County

The Yellowstone Gateway County.

Park County follows the Yellowstone River and Paradise Valley to Yellowstone's north entrance — a tourism, fly-fishing, arts, and ranching economy anchored by the historic railroad town of Livingston.

We serve businesses across the county — Livingston, and the smaller communities in between.

The MT Tax Angle

No general sales tax — and income to 5.9%.

Montana charges no general sales tax, so Park County businesses collect nothing at the register in most towns — the state runs on a graduated income tax that tops out at 5.9% (plus a corporate income tax), and a few resort communities add a local resort tax of up to 3%. We keep your income and any resort-tax records clean.
