📍 About Blaine County

Idaho's Resort County.

Blaine County is Idaho's premier resort region &mdash; home to Ketchum and Sun Valley, one of the country's original destination ski resorts, with an economy built on tourism, hospitality, high-end real estate, and the arts.

We serve businesses across the county — Ketchum, and the smaller communities in between.

The ID Tax Angle

A flat income tax — and a simple 6% sales tax.

Idaho pairs a flat 5.3% income tax (2026, individuals and corporations alike) with a simple 6% statewide sales tax, and across most of Blaine County there's no local sales-tax add-on at all — only a few resort cities levy one. That simplicity is a real advantage, and we keep your income, sales, and payroll taxes reconciled and filed on time.
