📍 About Houston, TX

The City Behind the Business.

Houston is more than a zip code — it's one of Texas's most dynamic cities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Founded in 1836 by brothers Augustus and John Allen, Houston was named after General Sam Houston, hero of the Battle of San Jacinto. The city briefly served as the capital of the Republic of Texas and grew as a railroad hub and cotton trading center through the 19th century.

The 1901 Spindletop oil gusher near Beaumont launched Houston into the petroleum age, and the city never looked back. The Houston Ship Channel (1914) connected the city to the Gulf of Mexico, creating the Port of Houston. NASA's Johnson Space Center (1961) made Houston synonymous with space exploration — "Houston, we have a problem" entered the global lexicon. The Texas Medical Center grew into the world's largest, Enron's collapse (2001) tested but didn't break the city, and Houston emerged as one of America's most diverse and economically dynamic metros.

Today that global energy continues — with more small business owners than ever, and more who need clean books to grow with confidence.
