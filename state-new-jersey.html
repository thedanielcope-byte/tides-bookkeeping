📍 About New Jersey

The Garden State.

New Jersey has one of the largest and most productive economies in the country, driven by pharmaceuticals and life sciences (long called the nation's medicine chest, home to Johnson & Johnson, Merck, and Bristol Myers Squibb), the massive Port Newark-Elizabeth complex, finance and insurance, telecommunications, and logistics serving the New York and Philadelphia metros. Dense, wealthy, and highly educated, the state packs enormous commercial activity between two major markets. Newark and Jersey City anchor its urban economy.

The NJ tax picture

New Jersey is a high-tax state. Its graduated personal income tax tops out at 10.75% on income above $1 million — among the highest rates in the nation (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). It also has the highest property taxes in the country, averaging well over 2% of home value, plus a 6.625% state sales tax (with clothing and most groceries exempt). Businesses face a Corporation Business Tax with a comparatively high top rate (as of 2026 — confirm current rate), so entity choice and clean records matter. In a state this tax-heavy, accurate books are less a luxury than a survival tool.
