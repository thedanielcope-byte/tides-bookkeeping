About the Area

Miami-Dade Is
Florida's Economic
Powerhouse.

Miami-Dade County is the most populous county in Florida and the economic engine of South Florida. Home to the City of Miami, it serves as the gateway to Latin America and the Caribbean, with a thriving international trade economy, world-class tourism industry, and rapidly growing tech sector.

With PortMiami handling more cruise passengers than any port in the world, Miami International Airport serving as the top US gateway for Latin American trade, and a booming startup scene in Brickell and Wynwood, the county generates enormous small business activity across every sector imaginable.

History & Economic Identity

Miami-Dade County's transformation from a sleepy resort town to one of the most globally connected metropolitan areas in the Western Hemisphere is one of the great economic stories of the 20th century. The arrival of Cuban exiles in the 1960s ignited an entrepreneurial revolution that continues today, making Miami one of the most business-dense cities per capita in the United States.

PortMiami is the cruise capital of the world, and Miami International Airport handles more international freight than any US airport — making the county a critical node in global supply chains. The Brickell Financial District has emerged as a serious challenger to traditional Wall Street firms, attracting hedge funds, fintech companies, and crypto businesses.

Today, Miami-Dade blends its Latin American cultural identity with a booming tech ecosystem, world-class healthcare institutions like Jackson Health System and Baptist Health, and an enormous tourism industry. That diversity creates a wide range of small business types — and a high demand for reliable bookkeeping.

Key Corridors & Communities

Brickell Financial District
Miami's Wall Street — dense with hedge funds, fintech firms, law offices, and financial services companies in gleaming high-rises.
Wynwood & Design District
Miami's creative epicenter — galleries, agencies, restaurants, boutiques, and tech startups in converted warehouses.
Doral / NW 36th St Corridor
The logistics and warehousing hub near MIA. Dense with freight forwarders, importers, distributors, and Latin American HQs.
Coral Gables / Miracle Mile
Corporate headquarters, professional services, law firms, and the University of Miami campus.
Hialeah / Medley Industrial
Manufacturing, distribution, and industrial services — one of the densest small business corridors in Florida.
Kendall / Dadeland
Suburban commercial hub with medical offices, retail centers, and professional services along US-1 and Kendall Drive.
Aventura / North Beach
Upscale retail, hospitality, and professional services along Collins Avenue and Biscayne Boulevard.
Homestead / Florida City
Agricultural businesses, logistics, and gateway to Everglades and Keys tourism industries.
