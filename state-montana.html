📍 About Montana

Big Sky Country — and No Sales Tax.

Montana's Big Sky Country economy is built on agriculture and ranching, energy and mining, and a booming outdoor-recreation and tourism sector fed by Glacier and Yellowstone National Parks. Billings is the largest city and a regional trade and healthcare hub, while Bozeman and Missoula have seen fast growth in technology, education, and professional services. Small businesses — from ranches to outfitters to tech startups — form the backbone of the state economy.

The MT tax picture

Montana is one of only a handful of states with no general sales tax, a real advantage for retailers and consumers alike (a few resort communities levy a local resort tax, and there are lodging and rental-vehicle taxes). It does have a personal income tax, recently simplified to two brackets with a top rate of 5.65% (as of 2026 — confirm current rate). There's no statewide sales tax to track and no gross-receipts tax, which keeps compliance relatively simple for most small businesses. Clean books still matter for income tax, payroll, and knowing your true margins.
