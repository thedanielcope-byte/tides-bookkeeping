Rentals in five states, one login, and books that stay current

Where you're starting

2025 is done. 2026 hasn’t been started.

You hold long-term rentals through a Wyoming holding LLC and an LLC in each state: Tennessee, Ohio, Arizona, Indiana and West Virginia, plus Pennsylvania, which you sold and are closing out this year. A California corporation collects one payment a month from the business you sold.

Your books are finished through December 31, 2025 and with your tax preparer. Your bookkeeper couldn’t continue into 2026, so nothing has been entered since January. Everything sits in QuickBooks Desktop, with a separate login for each company.

You told me what you want. Get 2026 caught up, move to QuickBooks Online so next year is simple, and have books that are current whenever you look. No monthly reports. One clean package for your CPA so your return is filed before April.

How I'd set it up

Four pieces

01
Remote access to your QuickBooks Desktop files
Your Desktop files live on your laptop, so the catch-up starts with remote access through Splashtop, as you offered. I use it to open each company, pull the December 31, 2025 balances, the chart of accounts and the vendor list, and see how your bookkeeper handled things. I only read and export. Nothing in the Desktop files is changed, so they stay as your record of 2025.
02
Each company gets its own books in QuickBooks Online
Separate LLCs in separate states need separate books, and your CPA will want them that way. You get one login, with a menu at the top to switch between companies. Each one opens on January 1, 2026 with the balances from Desktop, and its bank and card accounts connected.
03
2026 is entered once, in QuickBooks Online
I work one company at a time, starting with Pennsylvania because it’s closed, and saving Tennessee and the holding LLC for last because they carry the most activity. Each property manager’s statement is entered, the nine Tennessee mortgages are split between principal and interest, and the Pennsylvania sale is recorded from its closing statement.
04
The holding LLC gets sorted out
You ran your business through it until March or April. I’ll separate what belongs to the real estate from what belongs elsewhere, and send you a short list of anything I can’t place, including the card charges you said you don’t recognize.

Scope of work

What I’ll do

  • Catch-up and move to QuickBooks Online, January through October 2026 (one-time). Remote sessions into your Desktop files, each company set up in QuickBooks Online, every bank account and card reconciled for the ten months, property manager statements entered, mortgages split, the Pennsylvania sale and close-out recorded, and a short list of anything I need you to answer.
  • Ongoing every month, from November 1. Every account in every company reconciled, statements entered and mortgages split, so the books are current any day you open them. You don’t need to chase me for it.
