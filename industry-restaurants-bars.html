What Restaurant Bookkeeping Services Include

A restaurant's books are built from the POS, not the bank statement. This is the work behind every month, how often it happens, and what you get back.

What gets doneHow oftenWhat you get
Each day's POS sales are booked by category: food, beverage, liquor, comps, discounts, tips, sales tax and card feesWeeklySales in your books that tie to your Toast, Square or Clover reports
Bank and credit card accounts are reconciledKept current weekly, reconciled at month endA reconciliation report for every account
Food, beverage and liquor purchases are coded to separate cost-of-goods accountsWeeklyFood cost and beverage cost as a percent of sales
Payroll and tips are booked from your payroll providerEvery pay runLabor cost as a percent of sales, with tips owed kept out of revenue
Sales tax collected is tracked as a liability, not incomeWeeklyA sales tax balance that ties to what you file
Delivery app payouts (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub) are booked at gross, with commissions broken outEvery payoutTrue sales and the true cost of each delivery channel
The month is closedMonthlyA P&L by month, balance sheet, cash flow statement and a written analysis of the month
Prime cost (cost of goods plus labor) is calculatedMonthlyThe one number that shows whether the month worked
