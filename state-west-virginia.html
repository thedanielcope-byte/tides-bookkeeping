📍 About West Virginia

The Mountain State, Cutting Its Income Tax.

West Virginia's economy has long been rooted in energy - coal, natural gas from the Marcellus and Utica shale, and chemicals along the Kanawha Valley. In recent years the state has leaned into outdoor recreation and tourism (whitewater rafting and the New River Gorge National Park), healthcare, and higher education anchored by West Virginia University in Morgantown. Aggressive income-tax cuts are part of a broader push to attract new businesses and remote workers.

The WV tax picture

West Virginia is in the middle of a multi-year effort to shrink its income tax. The graduated individual income tax now tops out around 4.58% after a series of cuts from 6.5%, with additional reductions possible if revenue triggers are met (as of 2026 - confirm current rate before filing). The state sales tax is 6%, and a handful of municipalities add up to 1% more (about 6.6% on average). West Virginia repealed its old business franchise tax years ago and applies a flat 6.5% corporate net income tax, which makes the falling personal rate the headline story for small-business owners.
