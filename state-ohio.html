📍 About Ohio

A State Built on Making Things.

Ohio has one of the most diversified economies in the country — Intel's semiconductor "Silicon Heartland" and a deep finance and insurance sector in Columbus, world-class healthcare in Cleveland, consumer-goods giants in Cincinnati, aerospace and defense in Dayton, glass and auto in Toledo, and polymers in Akron.

Ohio's Economy & Business Climate

Ohio is a top-ten U.S. state economy built on manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, and a fast-growing tech base. It sits within a day's drive of more than half the U.S. population, which is why so much of the country's industry — auto, aerospace, glass, polymers, and consumer goods — has always run through the Buckeye State.

But Ohio's real bookkeeping challenge is local. The state has more than 600 municipal income taxes — meaning businesses must withhold local tax for every city and village where employees work, and file net-profit returns through RITA, the CCA, or self-administered cities like Columbus and Cincinnati. Multi-municipality withholding is the single most common headache we clean up for Ohio businesses — and keeping those books straight is exactly what we do.

🖥️

Key Interstate & Highway Infrastructure

Ohio's highway network ties its three "C" metros together and connects the state's factories to the rest of the country — from the Lake Erie ports to the Ohio River.

I-71 — The Three-C Spine
Connects Cleveland, Columbus, and Cincinnati diagonally across the state — Ohio's primary population and freight corridor.
I-75 — Western Freight Corridor
Toledo to Dayton to Cincinnati — the manufacturing and auto backbone of western Ohio.
I-70 — East–West Route
Runs across central Ohio through Columbus, Springfield, and Dayton — a major national truck route.
I-77 & I-76 — Northeast Ohio
Link Cleveland, Akron, and Canton through the industrial northeast and the polymer belt.
I-90 & the Ohio Turnpike
The I-90 / I-80 turnpike runs the Lake Erie shoreline through Cleveland and Toledo, linking the northern ports.
I-270 — The Columbus Outerbelt
The beltway circling Columbus, anchoring the region's explosive logistics and suburban growth.

The Ohio Tax Landscape

🧾
Municipal Income Tax & RITA
Ohio has 600+ city and village income taxes. Businesses withhold for every municipality where staff work and file net-profit returns through RITA (Regional Income Tax Agency), the CCA (Central Collection Agency), or self-administered cities like Columbus and Cincinnati.
📊
Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)
Ohio's gross-receipts tax. As of 2024–2025 the exclusion rose so only businesses with more than $3M in taxable gross receipts owe CAT — most small businesses are now exempt, but growing firms need to watch the threshold.
🛍️
State & County Sales Tax
Ohio's state sales tax is 5.75%, with county piggyback rates pushing the combined total to roughly 6.5%–8% depending on where you sell.
