📍 About Virginia

Government, Defense, and Data Center Alley.

Virginia's economy is powered by the federal government, defense, and technology, especially in the Northern Virginia suburbs of Washington, D.C. Loudoun County's 'Data Center Alley' routes a huge share of the world's internet traffic, while the Hampton Roads region is a hub for shipbuilding, ports, and the U.S. Navy. Richmond adds finance, law, and corporate headquarters, and the state also has deep roots in agriculture, tobacco, and higher education.

The VA tax picture

Virginia's individual income tax is graduated from 2% to a top rate of 5.75%, but because that top bracket begins at just $17,000 of taxable income, most working Virginians effectively pay the 5.75% rate (as of 2026 - confirm current rate before filing). The statewide minimum sales tax is 5.3% (4.3% state plus 1% local), rising to 6% in Northern Virginia and Hampton Roads and to 7% in the Central Virginia and Historic Triangle regions because of regional add-ons (as of 2026 - confirm current rate). Corporations pay a flat 6% corporate income tax. One quirk: Virginia businesses register with the State Corporation Commission rather than a Secretary of State.
