About the Area

St. Joseph County Is
Northern Indiana's
University City.

St. Joseph County is anchored by South Bend, Mishawaka, and the University of Notre Dame — creating one of the most economically dynamic metros in northern Indiana. The county's economy blends world-class education, healthcare, advanced manufacturing, and a growing tech sector.

Notre Dame's $18B endowment and 12,000+ students fuel a massive service economy. South Bend's revitalization under recent administrations has attracted tech investment, downtown development, and a growing restaurant and professional services scene. Mishawaka's retail corridors and Granger's affluent suburban growth round out a diverse business landscape.

History & Economic Identity

St. Joseph County's economy has been shaped by two forces: the University of Notre Dame and the Studebaker automobile legacy. While Studebaker closed in 1963, the industrial infrastructure it left behind helped attract AM General (Humvee manufacturer), Honeywell, and a network of advanced manufacturers.

Notre Dame is the county's most powerful economic engine — its $18B endowment, research programs, and alumni network generate enormous demand for professional services, hospitality, and real estate. Beacon Health System (Memorial Hospital) and Saint Joseph Health System anchor the healthcare sector.

Today, St. Joseph County is experiencing a renaissance. South Bend's Innovation Park, Renaissance District, and data center investments are attracting tech companies. Mishawaka's Grape Road corridor remains one of northern Indiana's strongest retail zones. And the county's manufacturing base continues to evolve with advanced materials and defense contractors.
