📍 About Lane County

The Southern Willamette Valley.

Lane County spans the southern Willamette Valley from the Cascades to the coast — home to the University of Oregon, a timber-and-wood-products heritage, a growing technology and food-and-beverage base, and PeaceHealth, anchored by Eugene and Springfield.

We serve businesses across the county — Eugene, Springfield, and the smaller communities in between.

The OR Tax Angle

No sales tax — and a high income tax.

Oregon charges no sales tax at all, but Lane County businesses still navigate a graduated income tax that reaches 9.9% and the statewide Corporate Activity Tax (0.57% on commercial activity over $1 million, with a deduction for labor or cost of goods). We keep your income and Corporate Activity Tax records clean.
