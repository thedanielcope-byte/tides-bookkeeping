📍 About Socastee, SC

The Community Behind the Business.

Socastee is more than a zip code — it's one of the Grand Strand's fastest-growing communities. Understanding its neighborhoods, roads, history, and economy is how we serve our clients better.

History & Economic Growth

Socastee — a name drawn from a Native American term — began as a rural community strung along the Intracoastal Waterway in Horry County. For generations it was best known for the historic Socastee Swing Bridge, a rare working swing bridge that carried local traffic across the Waterway.

As Myrtle Beach and the wider Grand Strand boomed, Socastee grew into one of the area's most populous suburbs. Today its economy is residential and small-business driven — retail and services along US-501 and SC-707, marine and boating businesses along the Waterway, and contractors serving a steady stream of new homes, all supporting the region's tourism economy just minutes east.

That growth means more small business owners than ever — and more small business owners who need clean books to grow with confidence.
