📍 About Middlesex County

The Connecticut River County.

Middlesex County follows the Connecticut River to Long Island Sound — anchored by Middletown, home to Wesleyan University, Middlesex Health, and an insurance and services base, plus the shoreline and river towns of the lower valley.

We serve businesses across the county — Middletown, and the smaller communities in between.

The CT Tax Angle

A high income tax — and a statewide sales tax.

Connecticut businesses in Middlesex County navigate a graduated income tax that reaches 6.99% and a 6.35% state sales tax that applies statewide with no local add-on (meals are taxed at 7.35%). Corporations also owe the state corporation business tax. We keep your income, sales, and corporate-tax records clean.
